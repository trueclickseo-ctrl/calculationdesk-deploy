10:Te66,# Friendship Calculator – Interactive Friendship Quiz Guide

Friendships come in all shapes and sizes—from childhood best friends who know every secret to supportive college roommates and fun workplace pals.

Our **Friendship Quiz** gives you and your friends an interactive way to reflect on your relationship dynamics and discover your friendship compatibility score.

---

### How the Friendship Quiz Works

To start the quiz:
1. Enter **Your Name** and your **Friend's Name** in the input panel.
2. Click **Start Quiz** to launch the 5-question interactive evaluation.
3. Select your answer for each question regarding communication, trust, crisis support, argument resolution, and core values.
4. View your final percentage score and custom relationship verdict profile.

---

### The Scoring Algorithm Explained

Unlike simple name hash games, your Friendship Quiz score depends on **how you answer the 5 questions**:

* **Option A**: 20 Points (Strongest connection / highest alignment)
* **Option B**: 15 Points (High alignment)
* **Option C**: 10 Points (Moderate alignment)
* **Option D**: 5 Points (Casual alignment)

$$\text{Friendship Score} = \sum_{i=1}^{5} \text{Points}(\text{Question } i)$$

Because there are 5 questions with options ranging from 5 to 20 points, final scores range between **25% and 100%**.

---

### Verified Worked Example: Sarah & Jessica

Let's trace a sample quiz run for **Sarah** and **Jessica**:

1. **Q1 (Communication)**: Selects Option A ("Daily — we talk or text constantly") $\rightarrow$ **20 Pts**
2. **Q2 (Support)**: Selects Option B ("Offer support and help when I have free time") $\rightarrow$ **15 Pts**
3. **Q3 (Trust)**: Selects Option A ("100% trust — we know each other's deepest secrets") $\rightarrow$ **20 Pts**
4. **Q4 (Conflict)**: Selects Option C ("Agree to disagree without holding grudges") $\rightarrow$ **10 Pts**
5. **Q5 (Foundation)**: Selects Option A ("Complete mutual trust, loyalty, and empathy") $\rightarrow$ **20 Pts**

$$\text{Total Score} = 20 + 15 + 20 + 10 + 20 = \mathbf{85\%}$$

* **Score Verdict**: **85% – Close Friends**
* **Profile Message**: *"Close Friends. A highly supportive relationship with great mutual care."*

---

### Score Brackets & Verdict Profiles

* **90% – 100% (Inseparable Best Friends)**: Built on absolute trust, constant communication, and deep loyalty.
* **70% – 89% (Close Friends)**: A highly supportive relationship with great mutual care.
* **50% – 69% (Good Friends)**: Shared pleasant times with good foundation, though communication may be periodic.
* **25% – 49% (Casual Acquaintances)**: Shared interests, but operating on distinct personal paths.

---

### Why Take the Quiz with Friends?

* **Great Conversation Starter**: Comparing your answers with a friend often leads to fun conversations about past memories and shared experiences.
* **Perception Comparison**: Have both friends take the quiz independently to see if your scores match!
* **Purely for Fun**: Remember that no online quiz measures the true emotional depth of a friendship. Enjoy the score as a fun game.
