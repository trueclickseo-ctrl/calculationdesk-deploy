What is the Scientific Calculator?

The TI-84 Style Scientific Calculator is a premium mathematical workspace modeled after the classic handheld calculator to compute trigonometric, logarithmic, algebraic, and exponential equations.

Formula & Calculation Method

Trigonometric and logarithmic formulas evaluated by this utility include: **1. Trigonometric Functions (radians):** $$\sin(\theta), \quad \cos(\theta), \quad \tan(\theta)$$ **2. Logarithmic Functions:** $$\text{Natural Log: } \ln(x) = \log_e(x), \quad \text{Common Log: } \log_{10}(x)$$
