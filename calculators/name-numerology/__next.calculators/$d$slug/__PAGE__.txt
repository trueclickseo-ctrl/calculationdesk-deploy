10:T1228,# Name Numerology Calculator – Pythagorean Destiny Number Guide

In Western numerology, your name is considered a powerful expression of identity. While your birth date reveals your Life Path, **Name Numerology** calculates your **Destiny Number** (or Expression Number) based on the letters of your name.

Enter your full name above to calculate your Pythagorean Destiny Number and view your letter-by-letter breakdown.

---

### What is Name Numerology?

Name Numerology translates written letters into numbers using mathematical conversion charts established by the Greek philosopher Pythagoras.

In numerology tradition, your Destiny Number represents:
* Your inherent talents and natural abilities.
* How you express yourself to achieve personal goals.
* Potential strengths and personal development opportunities.

*Note: Name numerology is a cultural and philosophical tradition, not a scientifically validated psychological measurement.*

---

### The Pythagorean Letter Value Chart

The Pythagorean system assigns numbers from 1 to 9 sequentially across the alphabet:

| Value | Corresponding Letters |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | A, J, S |
| **2** | B, K, T |
| **3** | C, L, U |
| **4** | D, M, V |
| **5** | E, N, W |
| **6** | F, O, X |
| **7** | G, P, Y |
| **8** | H, Q, Z |
| **9** | I, R |

---

### How the Calculation Works

1. **Text Formatting**: Capital letters are converted to lowercase, and non-letter characters (spaces, hyphens, numbers, punctuation) are filtered out.
2. **Letter Mapping**: Each character in the name is converted into its Pythagorean digit according to the chart above.
3. **Summation**: All letter digits are added together.
4. **Digit Reduction**: The sum is reduced by adding its digits together until a single digit ($1-9$) or a Master Number ($11, 22$) is produced.

---
