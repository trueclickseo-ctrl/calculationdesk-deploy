f:T798,# Lumpsum Calculator – One-Time Investment Growth Guide

A **Lumpsum Investment** involves depositing a single, one-time lump sum of money into mutual funds, stocks, or financial assets rather than spreading your capital over regular monthly installments. It is ideal for deploying windfalls, annual work bonuses, or asset sale proceeds.

---

### How One-Time Lumpsum Compounding Works

When you invest a lump sum, your entire capital starts earning returns immediately from day one. Compound interest builds upon both your original principal and previously accumulated gains each year.

#### The Mathematical Lumpsum Formula

A = P × (1 + r)^t

Where:
- **A (Maturity Amount)**: Expected future value of your investment.
- **P (Principal Amount)**: The initial one-time deposit.
- **r (Annual Expected Return Rate)**: Quoted annual return percentage divided by 100 (r = Return % ÷ 100).
- **t (Tenure in Years)**: Investment period in years.

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Example

Suppose you make a one-time deposit of **$50,000** for **10 years** at an expected annual return rate of **12%**:

1. **Convert Rate to Decimal (r)**:
 r = (12 / 100) = 0.12
2. **Apply the Lumpsum Formula**:
 A = 50,000 × (1 + 0.12)^10
 A = 50,000 × (1.12)^10
 A = 50,000 × 3.105848 ≈ \155,292.41 3. **Summary (Verified against Code)**:
 - **Invested Principal**: **$50,000**
 - **Estimated Wealth Gain**: \155,292.41 - $50,000 = $105,292.41
 - **Total Maturity Value**: **$155,292**

  * A1: An SIP involves investing smaller, fixed deposits every month over time, whereas a lumpsum investment deploys your entire capital up front in a single transaction.
