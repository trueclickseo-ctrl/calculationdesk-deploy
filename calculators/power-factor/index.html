What is the Power Factor Calculator?

Determine efficiency ratios in electrical systems.

Formula & Calculation Method

$$\text{Power Factor} = \frac{\text{Real Power (kW)}}{\text{Apparent Power (kVA)}}$$

Worked Example Calculation

8 kW real power and 10 kVA apparent power has a PF of 0.8.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is an ideal power factor?

A PF of 1.0 (unity) is ideal, indicating all electrical energy is converted to productive work.
