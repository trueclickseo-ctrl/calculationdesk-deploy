What is the Graphing Calculator?

The Graphing Calculator is a high-performance visual plotter built to render functions and algebraic equations dynamically on a fully interactive Cartesian coordinate plane.

Formula & Calculation Method

Curves are rendered by evaluating equations across continuous math coordinates: $$y = f(x)$$ Where $f(x)$ is solved for every column of pixels across the canvas, rendering smooth geometric paths.

Worked Example Calculation

To plot a parabola and sine wave: 1. Add y1 = x^2 2. Add y2 = sin(x) 3. Drag the graph area to pan and use your scroll wheel to zoom.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I zoom or move around the graph?

Simply click and drag anywhere on the graph grid to pan. You can zoom in and out using your mouse scroll wheel, or by clicking the overlay buttons in the top-right corner.

What mathematical functions are supported?

The plotter supports algebra (+, -, *, /), exponents (^), square roots (sqrt), and standard trigonometric and logarithmic functions (sin, cos, tan, log, ln).
