# CGPA to Percentage Calculator – Score Conversion Guide

When filling out college admission applications, employment forms, or scholarship requests, students are often required to convert their **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)** into an equivalent **percentage mark**, or vice versa. This calculator performs both forward and reverse conversions using a customizable board multiplier.

## How the Score Conversion Formulas Work

**Mode 1 — CGPA to Percentage:**

$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = \text{CGPA} \times \text{Multiplier}$$

**Mode 2 — Percentage to CGPA (reverse conversion):**

$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\text{Percentage (\%)}}{\text{Multiplier}}$$

Where **Multiplier** is the conversion factor specified by your educational board — the CBSE standard multiplier is 9.5, while some technical universities use 10.0 or another custom value.

## Worked Examples

**CGPA to Percentage** (8.8 CGPA under the CBSE 9.5 scale):

$$8.8 \times 9.5 = 83.60\%$$

**Percentage to CGPA** (80% mark under the CBSE 9.5 scale):

$$80 \div 9.5 \approx 8.42 / 10.0$$

## How the Multiplier Changes the Result

The same CGPA produces a noticeably different percentage depending on which board's multiplier is applied — this matters on forms with a strict minimum percentage cutoff:

| CGPA | Multiplier | Equivalent Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| 8.8 | 9.5 (CBSE) | 83.60% |
| 8.8 | 10.0 | 88.00% |

## What This Calculator Does Not Include

> [!NOTE]
> **Real-world exclusions**: This calculator applies a single linear multiplier to convert between scales. Some institutions instead use bracket-based or non-linear conversion tables rather than a flat multiplier, and this tool does not model those. Always confirm the exact conversion method your target institution accepts before submitting an official form.
