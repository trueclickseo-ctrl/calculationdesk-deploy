# Grade Calculator – Required Final Exam Score Guide

When preparing for end-of-term assessments, students need to know the **minimum score required on the final exam** to secure their target overall grade in a class.

This calculator computes your **required final exam score** and indicates whether your goal is achievable with standard scoring or requires extra credit.

---

### How the Required Exam Score Is Calculated

The relationship between your current coursework grade, final exam weight, and target final grade is governed by the weighted average equation:

Required Exam Score = [ Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Exam Weight) ] / Exam Weight

Where:
- **Current Grade**: Your cumulative percentage score prior to the final exam.
- **Desired Grade**: The overall target percentage you want to earn in the course.
- **Final Exam Weight**: The percentage of the total course grade assigned to the final exam (converted to decimal by dividing by 100).
- **1 − Exam Weight**: The percentage of the total course grade already completed through pre-final coursework.

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples (Verified against Code)

#### Example 1: Standard Target Case
Suppose your current grade is **85%**, your desired final grade is **90%**, and the final exam is worth **20%** of your total course grade:

1. **Convert Final Exam Weight to Decimal**:
   Exam Weight (w) = 20% / 100 = 0.20
   Coursework Weight (1 − w) = 1 − 0.20 = 0.80
2. **Calculate Current Coursework Contribution**:
   Contribution = 85 × 0.80 = **68.0%**
3. **Calculate Remaining Score Needed**:
   Remaining Needed = 90 − 68.0 = **22.0%**
4. **Calculate Required Final Exam Score**:
   Required Score = 22.0 / 0.20 = **110.0%**
5. **Result Interpretation**: You need a **110.0%** on the final exam. Because 110.0% exceeds 100%, reaching a 90% overall grade requires extra credit or bonus points.

#### Example 2: Exactly 100% Required Exam Score Case
Suppose your current grade is **80%**, your desired final grade is **85%**, and the final exam is worth **25%**:
- w = 0.25, 1 − w = 0.75
- Coursework contribution = 80 × 0.75 = 60.0%
- Remaining needed = 85 − 60.0 = 25.0%
- Required Score = 25.0 / 0.25 = **100.0%**
- **Result**: Scoring a perfect 100% on the final exam yields exactly an 85.0% overall course grade.

---

### Understanding the Key Factors

* **Current Grade vs. Target Grade**: The gap between your current grade and target grade determines how much ground the final exam must cover.
* **Impact of Final Exam Weight**: When the final exam carries a higher weight (e.g. 40% vs. 10%), each percentage point scored on the exam has a larger impact on your final course average.
* **What Scores Over 100% Mean**: The calculator evaluates the exact math. A result exceeding 100% indicates that standard perfect performance (100%) will fall short of your target, meaning extra credit is required.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: How is the required final-exam score calculated?**
  * A1: The calculator uses the weighted grade equation: Required Score = [Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Exam Weight)] / Exam Weight, where Exam Weight is entered as a decimal (e.g. 20% = 0.20).
* **Q2: What does a result above 100% mean?**
  * A2: A result above 100% means that under standard exam scoring (100% maximum), your current coursework average is not high enough to reach your target grade. You would need extra credit or bonus marks on the exam.
* **Q3: Does a higher final-exam weight reduce the required score?**
  * A3: If your desired grade is higher than your current grade, a higher final exam weight lowers the required exam score needed to lift your average.
