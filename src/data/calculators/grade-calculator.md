# Grade Calculator – Required Final Exam Score Guide

When preparing for end-of-term assessments, students need to know the minimum score required on the final exam to secure their target overall grade in a class. This calculator computes the required final exam score and indicates whether that goal is achievable with standard scoring or requires extra credit.

## How the Required Exam Score Is Calculated

The relationship between the current coursework grade, final exam weight, and target final grade is governed by the weighted average equation:

$$\text{Required Score} = \frac{D - C \times (1 - w)}{w}$$

Where **C** is the current grade prior to the final exam, **D** is the desired overall grade, and **w** is the final exam's weight as a decimal (its percentage divided by 100).

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Standard Target Case

Current grade **85%**, desired final grade **90%**, final exam worth **20%** of the total course grade:

1. Exam weight: $w = 20\% = 0.20$, coursework weight $= 1 - 0.20 = 0.80$
2. Coursework contribution: $85 \times 0.80 = 68.0\%$
3. Remaining needed: $90 - 68.0 = 22.0\%$
4. Required exam score: $22.0 \div 0.20 = 110.0\%$

Because 110.0% exceeds 100%, reaching a 90% overall grade requires extra credit.

### Example 2: A Second Extra-Credit Case

Current grade **82%**, desired final grade **88%**, final exam worth **30%**:

1. Exam weight: $w = 0.30$, coursework weight $= 0.70$
2. Coursework contribution: $82 \times 0.70 = 57.4\%$
3. Remaining needed: $88 - 57.4 = 30.6\%$
4. Required exam score: $30.6 \div 0.30 = 102.0\%$

Still slightly above 100%, meaning this target would also require a small amount of extra credit — showing that even a modest 6-point gap between current and desired grade can require more than a perfect exam score, depending on how much weight the final carries.

## How Exam Weight Changes the Required Score

The same 5-point gap between a current and desired grade produces very different required exam scores depending on how much weight the exam carries:

| Current Grade | Desired Grade | Exam Weight | Required Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80% | 85% | 25% | 100.0% (exactly achievable) |
| 85% | 90% | 20% | 110.0% (extra credit needed) |

## What This Calculator Does Not Include

> [!NOTE]
> **Real-world exclusions**: This calculator assumes the entered current grade already accounts for 100% of all non-final coursework, and that the final exam is the only remaining graded component. It does not model multiple remaining assignments with separate weights, or grading curves applied after the fact.
