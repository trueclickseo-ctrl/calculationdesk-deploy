# Macro Calculator – Flexible Macronutrient Distribution

While total calorie intake determines whether you gain, lose, or maintain weight, your **macronutrient distribution ("macros")** dictates the quality of those weight changes. Protein preserves lean muscle mass, carbohydrates fuel intense workouts, and healthy dietary fats support hormone production and cellular health.

This calculator converts your **daily target calorie budget** into exact gram targets for **Protein**, **Carbohydrates**, and **Fats** using standard energy densities and established diet protocol presets.

---

### What Are Macronutrients? The 4-4-9 Energy Rule

Macronutrients are nutrients your body requires in large daily quantities. Each macronutrient provides a specific amount of metabolizable energy per gram:

* **Protein ($4 \text{ kcal/g}$)**: Essential for muscle repair, enzyme production, immune defense, and satiety.
* **Carbohydrates ($4 \text{ kcal/g}$)**: The body's primary energy source for muscle glycogen and brain function.
* **Dietary Fats ($9 \text{ kcal/g}$)**: Dense energy source essential for cell membranes, brain tissue, and hormone synthesis.

---

### How Calorie Ratios Become Macro Grams

The calculator divides your total calorie budget using the target percentage split and converts energy into grams:

$$\text{Protein Grams} = \frac{\text{Daily Calories} \times \text{Protein \%}}{4}$$
$$\text{Carbohydrate Grams} = \frac{\text{Daily Calories} \times \text{Carbohydrate \%}}{4}$$
$$\text{Fat Grams} = \frac{\text{Daily Calories} \times \text{Fat \%}}{9}$$

---

### Diet Protocol Presets Explained

Different nutrition goals benefit from different macronutrient distributions:

| Diet Protocol | Protein % | Carbohydrate % | Fat % | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Balanced** | $30\%$ | $40\%$ | $30\%$ | General fitness, weight maintenance, sustainable healthy eating |
| **Low Carb** | $40\%$ | $25\%$ | $35\%$ | Insulin sensitivity improvement, moderate carbohydrate reduction |
| **High Protein** | $40\%$ | $30\%$ | $30\%$ | Fat loss while preserving muscle tissue, intense strength training |
| **Ketogenic** | $25\%$ | $5\%$ | $70\%$ | Strict ketosis, fat-adaptation, therapeutic dietary protocols |

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Example (Verified against Code)

Let's calculate the macro targets for a **2,000 calorie daily target** using the default **Balanced (30/40/30)** preset:

1. **Calculate Protein**:
   $$\text{Calories} = 2,000 \times 0.30 = 600 \text{ kcal}$$
   $$\text{Protein Grams} = \frac{600}{4} = \mathbf{150 \text{ grams}}$$
2. **Calculate Carbohydrates**:
   $$\text{Calories} = 2,000 \times 0.40 = 800 \text{ kcal}$$
   $$\text{Carb Grams} = \frac{800}{4} = \mathbf{200 \text{ grams}}$$
3. **Calculate Fat**:
   $$\text{Calories} = 2,000 \times 0.30 = 600 \text{ kcal}$$
   $$\text{Fat Grams} = \frac{600}{9} = 66.67 \text{ grams} \quad (\approx \mathbf{67 \text{ grams}})$$

*Verification Result: 150g Protein, 200g Carbs, 67g Fat ($150 \times 4 + 200 \times 4 + 67 \times 9 = 600 + 800 + 603 = 2,003 \text{ kcal}$, reflecting normal integer gram rounding).*

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Do I need to hit my exact macro numbers down to the gram every day?**
  * A1: No. Human eating is naturally variable. Aiming to hit within ±5 to 10 grams of your target macros is completely effective for long-term health and body composition goals.
* **Q2: Why are dietary fats calculated at 9 calories per gram instead of 4?**
  * A2: Fats are chemical triglycerides with denser carbon-hydrogen molecular bonds, yielding more than double the physiological energy density (9 kcal/g) of proteins or carbohydrates (4 kcal/g).
* **Q3: Should total calories or macros take priority?**
  * A3: Total daily energy intake (calories) determines overall weight change, while macronutrient distribution (especially protein intake) determines whether that weight change comes from fat loss or muscle tissue.
