### What Is Weight Conversion?

Weight and mass conversion is the process of expressing the same physical quantity in different units. Whether you're comparing a recipe from a US cookbook to your European kitchen scale, checking if a parcel meets a carrier's weight limit, or tracking your fitness progress between gym visits, the numbers only make sense when they're in the same unit.

This converter handles the seven most commonly used weight and mass units: milligrams (mg), grams (g), kilograms (kg), ounces (oz), pounds (lb), stone (st), and metric tonnes (t). Enter any value, select your starting unit and target unit, and the result appears instantly along with the full equivalency table.

### The Units Explained

**Metric system (grams as the base):**

Milligrams are used for very small quantities — pharmaceutical dosages, food nutrition labels, or laboratory measurements. There are 1,000 milligrams in one gram. Grams are the everyday cooking and baking unit throughout most of the world: 250 g of flour, 100 g of sugar, 28 g of protein per serving. Kilograms are the standard for body weight, grocery shopping, and freight in metric countries. One kilogram equals 1,000 grams. Metric tonnes (spelled with an 'e' to distinguish from the US short ton) are used for industrial quantities: vehicles, cargo, and agricultural yields. One metric tonne equals exactly 1,000 kg.

**Imperial/customary system:**

Ounces are used in the US for smaller weights in cooking, postal parcels, and products. One ounce = 28.3495 grams, and 16 ounces make one pound. Pounds are the primary everyday weight unit in the US — body weight, grocery packaging, and vehicle payloads. One pound = 453.592 grams. Stone is used almost exclusively in the UK and Ireland for body weight. One stone = 14 pounds = 6.350 kg.

### How the Conversion Works

The calculator converts everything through a gram-based reference:
1. Convert the input value to grams by multiplying by that unit's gram-equivalent.
2. Convert the gram total to the target unit by dividing by its gram-equivalent.

For example, converting 75 kg to pounds:
- 75 kg x 1,000 = 75,000 grams
- 75,000 / 453.592 = 165.347 lb

This two-step method works for any pair of units without needing a separate conversion factor for every possible combination.

### About Decimal Results

The converter displays results to up to 6 decimal places. This precision is useful for scientific and pharmaceutical work where small differences matter. For everyday purposes, rounding to 2 or 3 decimal places is perfectly adequate. Converting 75 kg to pounds gives 165.347 lb — for body weight purposes, 165.3 lb is just as useful.

### Common Mistakes

The most frequent source of confusion is mixing up ounces (a weight unit) with fluid ounces (a volume unit). A recipe calling for 4 oz of cream cheese means 4 ounces by weight (about 113 g), not fluid ounces. Similarly, people switching between the US and UK often misread kilograms as pounds: a person who weighs 70 kg does not weigh 70 lb — they weigh roughly 154 lb. Another common error is confusing metric tonnes with US short tons, which are about 10% lighter.
