# CTR Calculator

Enter the total clicks and impressions for an ad, email, or listing, and this calculator returns the click-through rate as a percentage.

## Formula

**CTR % = (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100**

For 50 clicks from 1,000 impressions: CTR = (50 ÷ 1000) × 100 = 5%.

## Why CTR benchmarks aren't one-size-fits-all

The "right" CTR depends enormously on where an ad is showing and to whom. Search ads reach people actively looking for something related, which naturally drives higher engagement than display ads shown passively to people browsing unrelated content. Comparing a display campaign's CTR against a search campaign's benchmark — or vice versa — will almost always look misleading, since the two formats start from very different baseline expectations for audience intent.

## CTR is a signal, not the whole story

A strong CTR tells you an ad is compelling enough to earn a click, and on platforms like Google Ads it can even lower what you pay per click by improving Quality Score. But CTR alone doesn't say what happens after that click — whether visitors convert, bounce immediately, or turn into paying customers. A campaign chasing CTR in isolation, without tracking what those clicks actually lead to, can end up optimizing for the wrong outcome.
