# Compatibility Calculator – Birth Date Relationship Index Guide

Determining how two people align is one of the oldest uses of natal astrology and numerology. Throughout history, traditions around the world have examined birth dates to compare personality archetypes and elemental energies.

Our **Compatibility Index** provides a multi-system analysis, evaluating your partnership across **Western Zodiac Elements**, **Chinese Zodiac Trines**, and **Numerology Life Paths**.

Enter both birth dates above to view your detailed multi-discipline compatibility breakdown.

---

### How the Triple-System Algorithm Works

Rather than relying on a single astrological sun sign, our component calculates an **Overall Compatibility Index** by averaging scores across three distinct traditional systems:

$$\text{Overall Index} = \frac{\text{Western Element Score} + \text{Chinese Trine Score} + \text{Numerology Life Path Score}}{3}$$

---

### 1. Western Zodiac Element Match
The 12 Western sun signs are grouped into four natural elements: **Fire**, **Earth**, **Air**, and **Water**. The calculator checks how the two elements interact:

* **Complementary Pairs (90% Score)**: Fire + Air (Air feeds Fire) or Earth + Water (Water nourishes Earth).
* **Same Elements (80% Score)**: Fire + Fire, Earth + Earth, Air + Air, or Water + Water.
* **General Pairings (70% Score)**: Mixed element combinations.
* **Challenging Pairings (45%–55% Score)**: Fire + Water (Water extinguishes Fire) or Earth + Air.

---

### 2. Chinese Zodiac Trine Branches
The 12 Chinese Zodiac animals are divided into four compatibility groups called **Trines**:

* **Trine Matches (95% Score)**: Animals separated by 4-year or 8-year gaps belong to the same compatibility trine (e.g. Rat, Dragon, Monkey; or Ox, Snake, Rooster) and share similar temperaments.
* **Opposition Clashes (40% Score)**: Animals separated by a 6-year gap sit opposite each other on the zodiac wheel, representing contrasting perspectives.
* **General Pairings (70% Score)**: Standard inter-animal pairings.

---

### 3. Numerology Life Path Match
The calculator derives each partner's single-digit Life Path Number from their birth date:

* **Identical Life Paths (95% Score)**: Both partners share the exact same Life Path number.
* **Same Polarity (85% Score)**: Both Life Path numbers share the same numerical polarity (both even numbers or both odd numbers).
* **Mixed Polarity (70% Score)**: One even Life Path and one odd Life Path.

---

### Verified Step-by-Step Worked Example

Let's calculate the compatibility index for:
* **Partner 1**: October 15, 1995 (`1995-10-15`)
* **Partner 2**: January 20, 1996 (`1996-01-20`)

#### Step 1: Western Element Compatibility
* Partner 1: Oct 15 $\rightarrow$ **Libra** (Air Element)
* Partner 2: Jan 20 $\rightarrow$ **Aquarius** (Air Element)
* Match: Air + Air (Same Elements) $\rightarrow$ **80%**

#### Step 2: Chinese Zodiac Compatibility
* Partner 1: Year 1995 $\rightarrow$ **Pig** (Index 11)
* Partner 2: Year 1996 $\rightarrow$ **Rat** (Index 0)
* Year Difference: $|11 - 0| = 11$ (Not a 4-year trine or 6-year clash) $\rightarrow$ **70%**

#### Step 3: Numerology Life Path Compatibility
* Partner 1 Life Path: $1995\text{-}10\text{-}15 \rightarrow 6 + 1 + 6 = 13 \rightarrow \mathbf{4}$ (Even)
* Partner 2 Life Path: $1996\text{-}01\text{-}20 \rightarrow 7 + 1 + 2 = 10 \rightarrow \mathbf{1}$ (Odd)
* Match: Even + Odd (Mixed Polarity) $\rightarrow$ **70%**

#### Step 4: Overall Index Calculation
$$\text{Overall Index} = \frac{80\% + 70\% + 70\%}{3} = \mathbf{73.3\%}$$

* **Final Result Card**: **73.3% Overall Index** (`Libra (Pig) & Aquarius (Rat)`)
