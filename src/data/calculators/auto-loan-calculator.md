# Auto Loan Calculator

Enter the vehicle price, your down payment, the interest rate, and the loan term, and this calculator returns the fixed monthly payment you'd owe on the amount financed.

## How the payment is calculated

**Monthly payment = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)**, where P is the amount financed (vehicle price minus down payment), r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments. This is the standard installment-loan formula — it spreads the financed amount across the term in a way that keeps the payment fixed even though the split between principal and interest shifts every month as the balance declines.

For a $25,000 vehicle with a $5,000 down payment, financed at 5.5% APR over 60 months, that works out to $20,000 financed and a monthly payment of about $382.02.

## Down payment and term both move the payment — differently

A bigger down payment reduces the amount financed directly, which lowers both your monthly payment and the total interest you'll pay, since interest accrues on a smaller balance every month. A longer term spreads that same financed amount across more payments, which lowers the monthly figure — but you end up paying interest for more months, so total interest paid over the life of the loan typically goes up even as the monthly payment goes down.

## What this doesn't include

This calculates principal and interest on the amount financed only. Real auto loans commonly also finance sales tax, title and registration fees, and sometimes dealer add-ons or extended warranties — all of which increase the actual amount financed beyond the base vehicle price, and with it the real monthly payment above what this calculator shows for the vehicle price alone.
