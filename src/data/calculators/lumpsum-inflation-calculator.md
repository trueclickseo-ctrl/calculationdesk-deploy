# Lumpsum Inflation Calculator – Real Purchasing Power Guide

When evaluating long-term investments, looking only at nominal cash returns can be misleading. **Inflation** erodes the buying power of money over time, meaning a large dollar amount in 20 or 30 years will buy far fewer goods and services than it does today.

This calculator computes both your **Nominal Future Value** (the raw dollar total) and your **Real Value** (the actual purchasing power in today's money terms).

---

### How Inflation Discounting Works

1. **Nominal Growth**: Your principal compounds annually at your expected investment return rate (R).
2. **Inflation Discounting**: The resulting nominal future cash value is discounted backward by your expected annual inflation rate (I) to isolate its true purchasing power.

#### Mathematical Calculation Methodology

#### Step 1: Nominal Future Value (FV)

Nominal Value = P × (1 + R)^t

#### Step 2: Real Future Value (Purchasing Power)

Real Value = frac(Nominal Value){(1 + I)^t}

#### Step 3: Loss Due to Inflation

Inflation Impact = Nominal Value - Real Value

Where:
- **P**: One-time principal investment.
- **R**: Expected annual return rate (Return % ÷ 100).
- **I**: Expected annual inflation rate (Inflation % ÷ 100).
- **t**: Investment duration in years.

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Example (Verified against Code)

Suppose you invest a **$50,000 lump sum** for **20 years**, expecting an **annual return of 12%** and an **annual inflation rate of 6%**:

1. **Calculate Nominal Future Value**:
 Nominal Value = 50,000 × (1 + 0.12)^20 = 50,000 × (1.12)^20 ≈ \482,314.392. **Calculate Real Value (Purchasing Power in Today's Terms)**:Real Value = (482,314.39 / (1 + 0.06)^20) = (482,314.39 / (1.06)^20) = (482,314.39 / 3.207135) ≈ $150,378.553. **Calculate Inflation Impact**:Inflation Impact = 482,314.39 - 150,378.55 = $331,935.84 4. **Summary**:
 - **Initial Deposit**: **$50,000**
 - **Nominal Maturity Value (Cash in 20 Years)**: **$482,314** (Nominal Gain: **$432,314**)
 - **Real Value (Purchasing Power in Today's Money)**: **$150,380** (Real Gain: **$100,380**)
 - **Purchasing Power Lost to Inflation**: **$331,936**

*Takeaway*: While your bank balance will display 482,314 in 20 years, that money will only buy what 150,380 buys today because inflation eroded 331,936 of buying power.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: What is the difference between nominal return and real return?**
  * A1: Nominal return is the raw percentage gain on your investment before considering inflation. Real return is the net percentage gain in your actual purchasing power after subtracting inflation.
