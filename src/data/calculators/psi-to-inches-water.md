# PSI to Inches of Water Converter

Enter a pressure value in PSI, and this calculator converts it into inches of water column (inH2O).

## Formula

**Inches of Water = PSI × 27.6799**.

1 PSI converts to 1 × 27.6799 ≈ 27.68 inches of water column.

## Why gas and HVAC pressures use inches of water

Residential and light commercial gas systems typically operate at pressures well under 1 PSI — too small a range to read precisely on a standard PSI gauge. Inches of water column, being a much finer-grained unit, gives technicians a more readable and precise scale at these low pressures, which is why natural gas line and HVAC static pressure specifications are almost always given in inH2O rather than PSI.

## Typical values in practice

Residential natural gas appliance connections in the US are commonly specified in the range of about 3.5 to 14 inches of water column, with roughly 7 inH2O being a common target at the appliance itself — though exact requirements vary by appliance and local code, so specific equipment documentation and jurisdiction rules should always be checked directly.

## A note on precision

Technically, the exact weight of a water column varies slightly with temperature, which can shift the precise conversion factor in very high-precision scientific contexts. For standard HVAC and gas fitting work, though, the conventional 27.6799 factor used here is the accepted standard and accurate enough for practical field use.
