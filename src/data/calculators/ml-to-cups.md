# ML to Cups Converter

Enter a volume in milliliters, and this calculator converts it into US customary cups.

## Formula

**Cups = Milliliters ÷ 236.588**, since one US cup is defined as exactly 236.588237 ml (8 US fluid ounces).

240 ml converts to 240 ÷ 236.588 ≈ 1.01 US cups — very close to, but not exactly, one full cup.

## US cups vs. metric cups

This calculator uses the US customary cup. Several other countries, including Australia and the UK, commonly use a metric cup instead, defined as exactly 250 ml — about 5.7% larger than the US cup. That gap is small enough to often go unnoticed in everyday cooking, but it's worth checking which "cup" a specific recipe means, especially for baking.

## Why the distinction matters more in baking

Baking depends on fairly precise ratios between ingredients — particularly flour to liquid — to get consistent texture and rise. A roughly 5% volume difference from using the wrong cup definition can shift those ratios enough to notice in the final result, which is why bakers in particular tend to be more careful about US-versus-metric cup measurements than cooks working on savory dishes.
