# Birth Year Calculator

Enter your current age and whether your birthday has passed this year, and this calculator finds your birth year along with your generational cohort and Chinese zodiac animal.

## Formula

If the birthday **has** passed this year: **Birth year = Current year − Age**. If it **has not** passed yet: **Birth year = Current year − Age − 1**.

For a 25-year-old in 2026 whose birthday hasn't yet passed: birth year = 2026 − 25 − 1 = 2000, which falls in the Generation Z range (1997-2012) and, following the 12-year zodiac cycle anchored to 1900 as a Year of the Rat, lands on the Year of the Dragon.

## Why birthday timing changes the answer

Age only increases the day a birthday happens. If this year's birthday hasn't occurred yet, the stated age still reflects last year's birthday — meaning the actual birth year is one earlier than "current year minus age" alone would suggest. That extra subtraction is the entire difference between the two formula branches.

## Generation and zodiac lookups aren't exact science

Generational labels are cultural conventions rather than official definitions — this calculator uses one commonly cited set of year ranges, but sources like Pew Research or the U.S. Census sometimes draw the boundary years slightly differently. The Chinese zodiac, by contrast, follows a fixed, well-defined 12-year repeating cycle, so once the birth year is known, the zodiac animal follows directly and unambiguously.
