# Fraction Calculator – Arithmetic & Mixed Number Guide

Working with fractions requires applying specific cross-multiplication and reduction rules depending on whether you are adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing.

This calculator solves fractional equations, returning **reduced proper fractions, mixed numbers, and decimal representations**.

---

### How Fraction Calculations Work

#### 1. Addition (+)
(a/b) + (c/d) = (a × d + b × c) / (b × d)

#### 2. Subtraction (−)
(a/b) − (c/d) = (a × d − b × c) / (b × d)

#### 3. Multiplication (×)
(a/b) × (c/d) = (a × c) / (b × d)

#### 4. Division (÷)
(a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a × d) / (b × c)

#### 5. Fraction Reduction & Mixed Numbers
- **GCD Simplification**: The resulting numerator and denominator are divided by their **Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)**.
- **Mixed Number Conversion**: If the simplified numerator is larger than the denominator (an improper fraction), the whole number portion is whole = trunc(numerator / denominator), and the remaining fractional part is remainder / denominator.

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples (Verified against Code)

#### Example 1: Addition (1/2 + 2/3)
1. **Apply Addition Formula**:
   (1 × 3 + 2 × 2) / (2 × 3) = (3 + 4) / 6 = **7/6**
2. **Simplify & Convert**:
   - GCD of 7 and 6 is 1 $\to$ **7/6**
   - Mixed Fraction: 7 divided by 6 is 1 with remainder 1 $\to$ **1 1/6**
   - Decimal Equivalent: 7 / 6 = **1.166667**

#### Example 2: Division (3/4 ÷ 2/5)
1. **Apply Division Formula**:
   (3 × 5) / (4 × 2) = **15/8**
2. **Simplify & Convert**:
   - Mixed Fraction: 15 divided by 8 is 1 with remainder 7 $\to$ **1 7/8**
   - Decimal Equivalent: 15 / 8 = **1.875**

---

### Denominator Restrictions

* **Zero Denominators**: Division by zero is undefined. Denominators `den1` and `den2` must not be zero.
* **Dividing by Zero Fraction**: When dividing (`÷`), the second numerator `num2` cannot be zero, as that would result in dividing by zero.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: How do you add fractions with different denominators?**
  * A1: Cross-multiply the numerators by opposite denominators, sum them for the new numerator, and multiply the denominators together: (a/b) + (c/d) = (ad + bc) / bd.
* **Q2: How does the calculator reduce fractions to lowest terms?**
  * A2: The calculator computes the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of the resulting numerator and denominator, then divides both numbers by that factor.
