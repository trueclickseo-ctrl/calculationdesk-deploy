# SIP Calculator – Monthly Systematic Investment Plan Guide

A **Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)** lets you invest a fixed sum of money at regular intervals (typically once a month) into mutual funds or investment portfolios. Rather than trying to time the market with a large one-time deposit, an SIP promotes disciplined, automated long-term wealth building.

---

### How Monthly SIP Compounding Works

When you invest through a monthly SIP, every installment buys mutual fund units at the current net asset value (NAV). Each monthly payment compounds for the remaining duration of your investment period.

#### The Mathematical Future Value (FV) Formula

$$FV = P \times \left[\frac{(1 + r)^n - 1}{r}\right] \times (1 + r)$$

Where:
- **$P$ (Monthly Investment)**: The fixed dollar amount deposited each month.
- **$r$ (Monthly Expected Return Rate)**: $\text{Annual Expected Return} \div 12 \div 100$.
- **$n$ (Total Number of Months)**: $\text{Investment Duration in Years} \times 12$.

*(Note: The factor $(1 + r)$ accounts for payments made at the beginning of each monthly compounding period).*

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Example

Suppose you invest **$5,000 every month** for **15 years (180 months)** at an expected annual return rate of **12%**:

1. **Calculate Monthly Return Rate ($r$)**:
   $$r = \frac{12}{12 \times 100} = 0.01$$
2. **Calculate Total Months ($n$)**:
   $$n = 15 \times 12 = 180\text{ months}$$
3. **Apply the SIP Formula**:
   $$FV = 5,000 \times \left[\frac{(1.01)^{180} - 1}{0.01}\right] \times 1.01$$
   $$FV = 5,000 \times \left[\frac{5.9958 - 1}{0.01}\right] \times 1.01$$
   $$FV = 5,000 \times 499.58 \times 1.01 \approx \mathbf{\$2,522,880}$$
4. **Summary**:
   - **Total Principal Invested**: $\$5,000 \times 180 = \mathbf{\$900,000}$
   - **Estimated Compounded Returns**: $\$2,522,880 - \$900,000 = \mathbf{\$1,622,880}$
   - **Total Maturity Value**: **$2,522,880**
