# Board Foot Calculator

Enter a board's thickness, width, and length, and this calculator returns its volume in board feet — the standard unit for pricing hardwood and specialty lumber.

## Formula

**Board Feet = (Thickness (in) × Width (in) × Length (ft)) ÷ 12**

For a 2x4 board that's 8 feet long: Board Feet = (2 × 4 × 8) ÷ 12 ≈ 5.33 FBM.

## Why hardwood is priced by volume, not length

Unlike standardized construction lumber, hardwood boards often come in varying widths and thicknesses even within the same grade and species, since they're cut from natural logs rather than milled to one uniform dimension. Board feet — a true volumetric measure — gives a fair, consistent basis for pricing regardless of how wide or thick any particular board happens to be, which is why hardwood suppliers quote by the board foot rather than simply by length.

## Nominal versus actual: a lumber industry quirk worth knowing

A "2x4" doesn't actually measure 2 inches by 4 inches once it's been dried and surfaced — its true, planed dimensions are closer to 1.5 by 3.5 inches. Board foot calculations, by long-standing industry convention, use the nominal (labeled) dimensions rather than these smaller actual dimensions, which can seem inconsistent at first but is universal practice — worth keeping in mind when comparing a hand-calculated board foot figure against a lumber yard's quote.
