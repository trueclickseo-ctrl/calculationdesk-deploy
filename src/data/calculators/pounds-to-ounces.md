# Pounds to Ounces Converter

Enter a weight in pounds, and this calculator converts it into the equivalent weight in ounces.

## Formula

**Ounces = Pounds × 16**, since one avoirdupois pound (the standard everyday pound) is defined as exactly 16 ounces.

5 pounds converts to 5 × 16 = 80 ounces.

## Not the same ounce used for precious metals

This calculator uses the avoirdupois system — the standard weight system for everyday goods in the US and UK. Precious metals like gold and silver instead use the troy system, where a troy pound contains only 12 (heavier) troy ounces. Applying this everyday conversion to a precious metal weight would give an incorrect result, since the two systems use different unit sizes entirely.

## Weight ounces vs. fluid ounces

It's worth distinguishing this weight-based ounce from the fluid ounce, which measures volume rather than mass. The two share a name but aren't the same kind of unit — a fluid ounce of a substance only weighs one ounce if that substance has roughly the density of water, which isn't true for most liquids, let alone solids.
