# Power-to-Weight Ratio Calculator

Enter a vehicle's or machine's horsepower and weight, and this calculator returns the power-to-weight ratio — a key indicator of acceleration potential.

## Formula

**Power-to-Weight Ratio = Horsepower ÷ Weight (lbs)**

For a 300 hp car weighing 3,000 lbs: Ratio = 300 ÷ 3000 = 0.10 hp/lb.

## Why this ratio beats raw horsepower for comparisons

Horsepower alone says nothing about how much mass that power has to move. A heavier vehicle with more horsepower can still accelerate more slowly than a lighter vehicle with less horsepower, if the lighter vehicle has the better power-to-weight ratio. Normalizing power by weight is what makes performance genuinely comparable across vehicles — or machines — of very different sizes.

## What the ratio doesn't capture

Power-to-weight ratio is a strong general indicator, but it isn't a precise acceleration predictor on its own. Drivetrain losses, tire grip, gearing, and aerodynamics all affect how efficiently that power actually translates into forward motion, so two vehicles with identical ratios can still perform noticeably differently in practice. Treat it as a useful comparison tool rather than a guaranteed performance figure.
