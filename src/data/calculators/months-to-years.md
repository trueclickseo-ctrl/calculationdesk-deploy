# Months to Years Converter

Enter a duration in months, and this calculator converts it into the equivalent number of years.

## Formula

**Years = Months ÷ 12**, since a calendar year always contains exactly 12 months.

45 months converts to 45 ÷ 12 = 3.75 years — 3 years and 9 months.

## Reading a decimal result

The whole-number portion of the result is complete years, and the decimal portion converts to months by multiplying it by 12. For 3.75 years, the 0.75 fraction times 12 gives 9 months, confirming "3 years and 9 months" as the full breakdown.

## Why this conversion is exact

Unlike day-based or week-based year conversions, which rely on an average year length to account for leap years, this one needs no averaging at all — a calendar year is always exactly 12 months, leap year or not, so dividing by 12 gives a precise result every time.
