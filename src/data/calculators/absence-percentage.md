# Absence Percentage Calculator

Enter the number of days absent and total scheduled work days, and this calculator returns the absence rate as a percentage.

## Formula

**Absence Rate % = (Absent Days ÷ Total Work Days) × 100**

For 5 days absent out of 100 scheduled work days: Absence Rate = (5 ÷ 100) × 100 = 5%.

## Why "healthy" absence rates vary so much

Most organizations aim to keep absence rate under roughly 1.5% to 3%, but that range shifts meaningfully depending on the industry and role — physically demanding jobs, seasonal illness patterns, and different leave policies all move the baseline. Comparing a specific team's or company's rate against a generic universal target is less useful than benchmarking against similar organizations or the same team's own historical trend over time.

## Planned time off versus genuine attendance problems

This calculation treats every absence the same way, but HR teams tracking real attendance issues usually want to separate the two: pre-approved vacation and personal days are expected, legitimate time away, while unplanned sick days and no-shows are what actually signal a potential problem worth investigating. Lumping both together into one blended absence rate can mask a genuine attendance trend that a more granular breakdown would reveal.
