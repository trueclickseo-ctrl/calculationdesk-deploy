# Audiobook Speed Calculator

Enter an audiobook's normal runtime and a playback speed, and this calculator finds your adjusted listening time and how much time that speed saves.

## Formula

**Adjusted listening time = Normal duration ÷ Speed multiplier**. **Time saved = Normal duration − Adjusted listening time**.

For a 12-hour (720-minute) audiobook at 1.5x speed: adjusted listening time = 720 ÷ 1.5 = 480 minutes (8 hours), saving 240 minutes (4 hours) off the original runtime.

## Why time savings aren't linear with speed

Each speed increase saves a progressively smaller amount of additional time. Going from 1x to 1.5x on a 12-hour book saves 4 hours, but going from 1.5x to 2x only saves 2 more hours — the remaining duration keeps shrinking, so the same speed jump produces a smaller absolute time savings each time.

## Where the "extra speed" comes from

Professional narrators typically speak around 150-160 words per minute — meaningfully slower than typical silent reading speed of 200-250 WPM. That gap is a big part of why many listeners can comfortably bump playback up to 1.25x-1.5x without feeling like they're missing anything, since the narration has room to run faster before it starts to outpace easy comprehension.

## Time math vs. comprehension

This calculator only computes the raw time savings — it doesn't measure whether you're actually retaining the content at a given speed. Most listeners find 1.25x-1.5x sounds natural, while 2x is more often used for re-listens or quick reviews of familiar material than for a first pass through something new and dense.
