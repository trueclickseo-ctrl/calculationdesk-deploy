# Days to Months Converter

Enter a duration in days, and this calculator converts it into the equivalent number of months.

## Formula

**Months = Days ÷ 30.437**, where 30.437 is the average number of days in a calendar month (365.2425 ÷ 12).

180 days converts to 180 ÷ 30.437 ≈ 5.91 months — almost exactly 6 full months.

## Why not just divide by 30?

Real calendar months range from 28 to 31 days, so no fixed number represents them exactly. Dividing by a plain 30 is a common shortcut, but it introduces a small error that compounds over longer durations. The 30.437-day figure instead reflects the true average month length across a full year (including the leap year cycle), giving a more accurate result for longer conversions like lease terms or multi-month project timelines.

## When to reach for a date-difference calculator instead

This conversion works well for translating a general day-count into an approximate number of months. But if you need the exact span between two specific calendar dates — accounting for which actual months and how many days each one has — a date-difference calculator that works directly from those two dates will give a more precise result than this average-based conversion.
