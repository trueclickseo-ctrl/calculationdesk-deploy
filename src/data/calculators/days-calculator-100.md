# 100 Day Calculator

Enter a starting date, and this calculator instantly finds the exact calendar date 100 days after and 100 days before it.

## Formula

**Future date = Starting date + 100 days**. **Past date = Starting date − 100 days**. The calculation works from actual calendar date objects, so it correctly handles month-length differences and leap years.

For January 1, 2026: 100 days in the future is April 11, 2026, and 100 days in the past is September 23, 2025.

## Where the "first 100 days" benchmark comes from

The idea traces back to Franklin D. Roosevelt's first 100 days in office in 1933, when his administration pushed through a wave of early New Deal legislation. The framing stuck as a media shorthand for judging a new president's early momentum, and it's since spread informally to new CEOs, managers, and other leadership roles as a round-number checkpoint — though unlike 30, 60, or 90-day periods, it's a cultural convention rather than anything with specific legal weight.

## 100 days vs. "about three months"

Three calendar months typically run 89 to 92 days depending on the specific months involved, so a 100-day count generally lands a week to a week and a half past a simple "three months from now." Because this calculator works from real calendar dates, it stays precise regardless of which months or year boundary the 100-day span happens to cross.
