# 120 Day Calculator

Enter a starting date, and this calculator instantly finds the exact calendar date 120 days after and 120 days before it.

## Formula

**Future date = Starting date + 120 days**. **Past date = Starting date − 120 days**. The calculation works from actual calendar date objects, so it correctly handles month-length differences and leap years.

For January 1, 2026: 120 days in the future is May 1, 2026, and 120 days in the past is September 3, 2025.

## Where 120-day windows come from

Government agencies commonly cite 120 days as part of a processing time estimate for visas, immigration petitions, and similar applications — usually as an outer bound in a published range rather than a guarantee, since actual processing can run faster or slower depending on volume and case complexity. The interval also appears in some construction bid bond validity periods and federal contract protest deadlines.

## 120 days vs. "four months"

These are close but not always identical — four consecutive calendar months can run 120 to 123 days depending on which months are involved and whether a leap year falls within the span. Because this calculator works from real calendar dates, the result stays precise regardless of exactly which months or year boundary the 120-day period crosses.
