# NPS Calculator – National Pension System Retirement & Pension Guide

The **National Pension System (NPS)** is a voluntary, long-term retirement savings scheme regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). It is designed to assist working professionals in accumulating a dedicated retirement corpus while securing a regular monthly pension after age 60.

---

### How NPS Investment & Payout Structure Works

During your working years, you make regular monthly contributions into your NPS account. The fund manager invests these contributions across equity, corporate bonds, and government securities.

Upon reaching **retirement age (60 years)**, your accumulated corpus is divided into two distinct components:
1. **Lump-Sum Withdrawal (Max 60%)**: You can withdraw up to 60% of the total accumulated wealth.
2. **Annuity Purchase (Min 40%)**: At least 40% of the corpus must be reinvested with an annuity service provider to pay a guaranteed monthly pension for life.

---

### The Mathematical NPS Calculation Formulas

#### Step 1: Total Accumulated Corpus ($A$) at Age 60

$$A = P \times \frac{(1 + i)^n - 1}{i} \times (1 + i)$$

Where:
- **$P$**: Monthly contribution amount.
- **$i$**: Monthly rate of return ($\text{Annual Expected Return} \div 12 \div 100$).
- **$n$**: Total investment months ($[60 - \text{Current Age}] \times 12$).

#### Step 2: Corpus Division & Monthly Pension

- **Annuity Corpus**:
  $$\text{Annuity Value} = A \times \left(\frac{\text{Annuity Percentage}}{100}\right)$$
- **Lump-Sum Payout**:
  $$\text{Lump Sum Value} = A - \text{Annuity Value}$$
- **Estimated Monthly Pension**:
  $$\text{Monthly Pension} = \frac{\text{Annuity Value} \times \left(\frac{\text{Annuity Rate}}{100}\right)}{12}$$

---

### Worked NPS Numerical Example

Suppose a **30-year-old individual** contributes **$5,000 per month**, expects an average **10% annual return**, and plans to allocate **40% for annuity at a 6% annuity rate** upon reaching age 60:

1. **Investment Duration**: $60 - 30 = 30\text{ years}$ ($n = 360\text{ months}$).
2. **Monthly Rate ($i$)**: $10\% \div 12 \div 100 = 0.008333\text{ per month}$.
3. **Accumulated Retirement Corpus ($A$)**:
   $$A = 5,000 \times \frac{(1 + 0.008333)^{360} - 1}{0.008333} \times (1 + 0.008333) \approx \$11,396,600$$
4. **Summary Breakdown at Age 60**:
   - **Total Principal Invested**: $\$5,000 \times 360 = \mathbf{\$1,800,000}$
   - **Total Wealth Growth**: $\$11,396,600 - \$1,800,000 = \mathbf{\$9,596,600}$
   - **Lump-Sum Cash Payout (60%)**: $\$11,396,600 \times 0.60 = \mathbf{\$6,837,960}$
   - **Annuity Corpus (40%)**: $\$11,396,600 \times 0.40 = \mathbf{\$4,558,640}$
   - **Estimated Monthly Pension**: $(\$4,558,640 \times 0.06) \div 12 = \mathbf{\$22,793\text{ / month}}$

---

### Key NPS Parameters & Rules

- **Mandatory Annuity Minimum**: Standard regulations mandate that subscribers allocate a minimum of 40% of their retirement corpus to an annuity.
- **Lump-Sum Limit**: Subscribers can withdraw up to 60% of their accumulated wealth.
- **Return Rate Variations**: Equity asset classes offer higher historical growth, whereas government bonds offer stability. Combining asset classes optimizes risk-adjusted returns over 20–30 year horizons.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Can I choose to allocate 100% of my NPS corpus to purchase an annuity?**
  * A1: Yes. The 40% rule is a minimum floor. You can allocate any proportion between 40% and 100% to annuity to generate higher monthly pension income.

* **Q2: Does the calculator account for market volatility?**
  * A2: The calculator projects nominal future wealth based on a constant expected annual growth rate. Actual portfolio value fluctuates over time.
