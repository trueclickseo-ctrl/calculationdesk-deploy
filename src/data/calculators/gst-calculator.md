# GST Calculator – Goods and Services Tax Inclusive & Exclusive Guide

The **Goods and Services Tax (GST)** is a multi-stage, destination-based indirect tax levied on the sale of goods and services. It is designed to replace multiple indirect taxes with a unified, transparent consumption tax system.

---

### How GST Calculations Work

GST calculations generally fall under two operational modes:

1. **Tax Exclusive (Add GST)**: The base price of the product does not include tax. Tax is calculated as a percentage of the base price and added to derive the final retail price.
2. **Tax Inclusive (Remove GST)**: The advertised price already includes tax. The pre-tax net cost and tax component are extracted by reversing the tax multiplier.

---

### Mathematical GST Formulas

#### 1. Adding GST (Tax-Exclusive Price)

$$\text{GST Amount} = \text{Base Price} \times \left(\frac{\text{GST Rate}}{100}\right)$$

$$\text{Total Price (Gross)} = \text{Base Price} + \text{GST Amount}$$

#### 2. Removing GST (Tax-Inclusive Price)

$$\text{Base Price (Pre-Tax)} = \frac{\text{Total Price}}{1 + \left(\frac{\text{GST Rate}}{100}\right)}$$

$$\text{GST Amount} = \text{Total Price} - \text{Base Price (Pre-Tax)}$$

---

### Worked Numerical Examples

#### Example 1: Add 18% GST (Exclusive) on a $1,000 Base Product
1. **Inputs**: Base Price = **$1,000**, GST Rate = **18%**.
2. **GST Amount**: $\$1,000 \times \left(\frac{18}{100}\right) = \mathbf{\$180.00}$
3. **Total Price**: $\$1,000 + \$180.00 = \mathbf{\$1,180.00}$

#### Example 2: Remove 18% GST (Inclusive) from a $1,000 Final Bill
1. **Inputs**: Total Price = **$1,000**, GST Rate = **18%**.
2. **Pre-Tax Base Price**:
   $$\text{Base Price} = \frac{1,000}{1 + 0.18} = \frac{1,000}{1.18} \approx \mathbf{\$847.46}$$
3. **GST Amount Extracted**:
   $$\text{GST Amount} = 1,000 - 847.46 = \mathbf{\$152.54}$$

---

### Understanding Indian GST Structure (CGST, SGST, IGST)

In dual GST systems like India's:
- **Intra-State Transactions (Within the same state)**: Tax is split equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For an 18% rate, **9% is CGST** and **9% is SGST**.
- **Inter-State Transactions (Between different states)**: Integrated GST (**18% IGST**) is collected by the central government and distributed to the destination state.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Why can't I just subtract 18% from an inclusive price to remove GST?**
  * A1: Subtracting 18% from $1,000 yields $820, which is incorrect because 18% tax was calculated on the smaller base price, not the final total. Dividing by 1.18 accurately isolates the original base price ($847.46).

* **Q2: Does this calculator support non-standard GST rates?**
  * A2: Yes. You can enter any valid percentage rate (e.g., 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%, or custom international VAT/sales tax rates).
