# ML to OZ Converter

Enter a volume in milliliters, and this calculator converts it into US fluid ounces.

## Formula

**Fluid Ounces = Milliliters × 0.033814**, since one US fluid ounce is defined as exactly 29.5735295625 milliliters.

500 ml converts to 500 × 0.033814 ≈ 16.91 US fluid ounces.

## US vs. UK fluid ounces

This calculator uses the US fluid ounce (about 29.5735 ml), the standard used in the United States and most international recipe and commerce contexts. The UK Imperial fluid ounce is slightly smaller, at exactly 28.4130625 ml — applying this US-based conversion to a UK-labeled product will give a slightly inaccurate result, so it's worth checking which standard a specific measurement is using.

## Fluid ounces aren't the same as dry ounces

Fluid ounces measure volume, while the more familiar "ounce" used on a kitchen scale measures weight — the two share a name but describe fundamentally different things. A fluid ounce of honey weighs more than a fluid ounce of water, since the fluid ounce measures how much space the liquid takes up, not how much it weighs.
