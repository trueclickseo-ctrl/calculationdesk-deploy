# FTE Calculator

Enter total part-time hours and the organization's full-time hour standard, and this calculator returns the equivalent FTE count.

## Formula

**FTE = Part-Time Hours per Week ÷ Full-Time Standard Hours per Week**

For 80 combined part-time hours against a 40-hour full-time standard: FTE = 80 ÷ 40 = 2.0.

## Why FTE beats raw headcount for staffing math

Headcount just tells you how many people are on payroll, without saying anything about how much work capacity that represents. FTE standardizes everything into "how many full-time-equivalent positions does this actually represent," which is why two teams with identical headcounts can have very different real staffing capacity if one leans heavily on part-time workers — FTE captures that difference where a simple headcount can't.

## A caveat worth knowing before using FTE for benefits or legal thresholds

This calculator uses a simple, general-purpose weekly-hours ratio, which is fine for internal budgeting and staffing comparisons. But certain legal and regulatory contexts — most notably the Affordable Care Act's employer mandate in the US — define FTE using their own specific counting rules, often based on average monthly hours rather than a simple weekly ratio. If an FTE figure is being calculated to determine legal or benefits eligibility, it's worth checking the specific regulatory definition rather than relying on this general formula.
