# Discount Calculator – Primary & Stacking Discounts with Tax

Shopping sales, clearance racks, and promotional events can save significant money. However, determining the final price at checkout can get confusing—especially when stores allow you to **stack a promotional coupon** on top of an already discounted sale item, or when local **sales tax** is added at payment.

A common shopping misconception is adding percentages together. A 20% store sale combined with an extra 10% loyalty coupon does **not** equal 30% off.

This calculator computes **Primary Discount Amounts ($)**, **Stacked Discount Amounts ($)**, **Pre-Tax Sale Prices ($)**, **Total Savings ($ & %)**, and the **Final Price with Sales Tax ($)**.

---

### Core Shopping Definitions

* **Original Price**: The initial retail sticker price or MSRP before any markdowns.
* **Primary Discount (%)**: The main storewide sale or clearance markdown percentage.
* **Stacking Discount (%)**: An optional secondary coupon (e.g. promo code, rewards membership, or clearance extra % off) applied after the primary discount.
* **Pre-Tax Sale Price**: The discounted price of the item before adding local sales tax.
* **Total Savings**: The cumulative dollar amount saved compared to the original price.
* **Final Price with Tax**: The total cash amount charged at the register.

---

### How Sequential Stacked Discounts Are Calculated

The calculator processes discounts **sequentially** (one after the other), matching real-world retail checkout systems:

#### Step 1: Calculate Primary Discount & Intermediate Price
Primary Discount Amount = Original Price  (P) ×  frac(Primary %){100} 
Primary Sale Price = P - Primary Discount Amount

#### Step 2: Calculate Sequential Stacking Discount
The secondary coupon applies to the *already reduced* primary price:
Stacked Discount Amount = Primary Sale Price ×  frac(Stacking %){100} 
Pre-Tax Sale Price = Primary Sale Price - Stacked Discount Amount

#### Step 3: Calculate Total Savings & Effective Percentage
Total Savings ($) = Original Price - Pre-Tax Sale Price
Effective Discount (%) = (Total Savings / Original Price) × 100

#### Step 4: Apply Sales Tax
Tax Amount = Pre-Tax Sale Price × (Tax Rate % / 100)
Final Price with Tax = Pre-Tax Sale Price + Tax Amount

---

### Why Two 20% Discounts Do NOT Equal 40% Off

Imagine an item priced at **$100**:
* **Sequential Stacking (Real Retail Math)**:
  - First 20% off: Saves \20 implies Price becomes \80.
  - Second 20% off: Saves 20% of \80 = $16 implies Final price becomes **\64**.
  - Total savings: \36 (**36% effective discount**).
* **Additive Fallacy (Incorrect Math)**:
  - Adding 20% + 20% = 40% off implies Saves \40 implies Price becomes **\60**.

Because the second 20% discount applies to a smaller base (\80 instead of \100), the second discount yields \16 in savings rather than \20.

### Practical Retail Shopping Tips

* **Compare Dollar Savings vs. Percentage**: A 50% discount on a 10 item saves 5, whereas a 10% discount on a 500 appliance saves 50. Always evaluate dollar savings alongside percentages.
* **Store Credit Rules**: Some retailers apply dollar-off coupons (e.g. "$10 off") *before* percentage promo codes, while others apply them *after*. Checking the sequence helps verify your register receipt.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Why doesn't taking 20% off and then 10% off equal 30% off?**
  * A1: Because stacked discounts apply sequentially. The primary 20% discount reduces a 100 item to 80. The secondary 10% coupon is then calculated on the NEW 80 price (saving 8), not the original 100 price. This results in 28 of total savings (a 28% effective discount), not 30%.
* **Q2: Is sales tax calculated on the original price or the discounted price?**
  * A2: In standard retail transactions, sales tax is assessed on the final discounted sale price that you actually pay ($72.00), rather than the original manufacturer MSRP ($100.00).
* **Q3: What is the difference between Sale Price, Discount Amount, and Total Savings?**
  * A3: Discount amount is the dollar reduction of a single offer (e.g. 20 off). Sale Price is the reduced price before tax ($72.00). Total Savings is the cumulative dollar amount saved across all combined discounts ($28.00).
