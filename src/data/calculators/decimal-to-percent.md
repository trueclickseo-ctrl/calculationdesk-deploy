# Decimal to Percent Calculator

Enter any decimal number, and this calculator converts it into its equivalent percentage.

## Formula

**Percent = Decimal × 100**

For 0.85: Percent = 0.85 × 100 = 85.0%.

## Why multiplying by 100 is the whole trick

"Percent" literally means "per hundred" — so converting a decimal to a percentage is just rescaling a value that's measured out of 1 into the equivalent value measured out of 100. A decimal like 0.85 already represents 85 hundredths of a whole; multiplying by 100 doesn't change that underlying proportion at all, it just expresses it on the more familiar hundred-based scale that "percent" refers to.

## What happens above 1.0

There's nothing special that happens at the boundary of "the whole thing" — a decimal of exactly 1.0 converts cleanly to 100%, and any decimal larger than that (representing more than the whole) converts to a percentage over 100%, just as correctly. A decimal of 1.5, for instance, becomes 150%, a perfectly valid result whenever a quantity has grown beyond its original starting point.
