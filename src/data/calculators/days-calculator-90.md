# 90 Day Calculator

Enter a starting date, and this calculator instantly finds the exact calendar date 90 days after and 90 days before it.

## Formula

**Future date = Starting date + 90 days**. **Past date = Starting date − 90 days**. The calculation works from actual calendar date objects, so it correctly handles month-length differences and leap years.

For January 1, 2026: 90 days in the future is April 1, 2026, and 90 days in the past is October 3, 2025.

## 90 days vs. "one quarter"

These are close but not identical. A calendar quarter runs 90 to 92 days depending on which specific months it covers and whether a leap year is involved, while this calculator always applies a fixed 90-day offset regardless of the months that span crosses. For most practical purposes they land near the same date, but not always exactly.

## Where 90-day periods come from

New-employee probationary periods of 90 days are common in US workplaces, though the length is set by individual company policy rather than any federal legal requirement — employers commonly use 60, 90, or 180 days instead. The interval also shows up in routine US passport processing estimates, many product warranty terms, and HIPAA breach notification deadlines, making it one of the more frequently used standard periods across both business and government processes.
