# Probability Calculator – Event Likelihood & Odds Guide

Understanding **probability and odds** allows students, statisticians, and researchers to quantify uncertainty and analyze event outcomes.

This calculator supports **Single Event Mode** and **Multiple Joint Events Mode**, providing complete calculations for success ratios, odds, unions, and conditional probabilities.

---

### How Probability Calculations Work

#### 1. Single Event Mode
- **Probability Ratio (P(A))**:
  P(A) = Favorable Outcomes (Successes) / Total Possible Outcomes (Trials)
- **Percentage Probability**:
  Percentage (%) = P(A) × 100
- **Failure Outcomes**:
  Failures = Total Outcomes − Favorable Outcomes
- **Success Odds**:
  Odds = Successes : Failures

#### 2. Multiple Joint Events Mode
Given P(A), P(B), and intersection P(A cap B):
- **Union P(A cup B) (Either A or B occurs)**:
  P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)
- **Conditional Probability P(A|B) (A given B occurred)**:
  P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
- **Conditional Probability P(B|A) (B given A occurred)**:
  P(B|A) = P(A ∩ B) / P(A)

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples (Verified against Code)

#### Example 1: Single Event (1 Favorable Outcome out of 6 Total)
Suppose you roll a standard 6-sided die aiming to land on a 4:
1. **Inputs**: Favorable Outcomes = 1, Total Outcomes = 6.
2. **Calculate Probability Ratio**:
 P(A) = 1 / 6 = **0.1667 (16.67%)**
3. **Calculate Failure Outcomes & Odds**:
 - Failures = 6 − 1 = **5**
 - Success Odds = **1:5** (1 win for every 5 losses)

#### Example 2: Joint Events (P(A)=0.50, P(B)=0.40, P(A cap B)=0.15)
1. **Calculate Union P(A cup B)**:
 P(A ∪ B) = 0.50 + 0.40 − 0.15 = **0.7500 (75.0%)**
2. **Calculate Conditional P(A|B)**:
 P(A|B) = 0.15 / 0.40 = **0.3750 (37.5%)**
3. **Calculate Conditional P(B|A)**:
 P(B|A) = 0.15 / 0.50 = **0.3000 (30.0%)**

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: What is the difference between Probability and Odds?**
  * A1: Probability is the ratio of favorable outcomes to all possible outcomes (e.g., 1 out of 6 = 16.67%). Odds is the ratio of favorable outcomes to unfavorable outcomes (e.g., 1 to 5, meaning 1 win for every 5 losses).
* **Q2: How is conditional probability P(A|B) calculated?**
  * A2: Conditional probability P(A|B) represents the probability of event A occurring given that event B has already occurred: P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B).
