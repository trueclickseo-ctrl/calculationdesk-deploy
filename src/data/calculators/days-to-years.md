# Days to Years Converter

Enter a duration in days, and this calculator converts it into the equivalent number of years.

## Formula

**Years = Days ÷ 365.2425**, where 365.2425 is the mean length of a Gregorian calendar year, accounting for the leap year cycle.

1,000 days converts to 1,000 ÷ 365.2425 ≈ 2.7379 years — about 2 years, 8 months, and 26 days.

## Why 365.2425 instead of a flat 365

A standard year has 365 days and a leap year has 366, adding up to an average of 365.2425 days per year once the Gregorian calendar's leap year rules are factored in. That figure closely tracks the actual solar year (about 365.2422 days), which is why it's used here instead of a flat 365-day assumption — the difference is small per year but compounds noticeably over longer durations.

## A different convention in finance

Some financial calculations intentionally use a simplified 360-day year (the 30/360 day-count convention) to make certain interest formulas easier to compute. That's a deliberate simplification used for specific financial contexts, distinct from the calendar-accurate 365.2425-day average this calculator uses for general date-to-years conversions.
