# Hours to Days Converter

Enter a duration in hours, and this calculator converts it into the equivalent number of days.

## Formula

**Days = Hours ÷ 24**, since a standard day is defined as exactly 24 hours.

78 hours converts to 78 ÷ 24 = 3.25 days, or 3 days and 6 hours.

## Reading a decimal result

The whole number portion of the result is complete days, and the decimal portion is a fraction of a day. To convert that fraction back into hours, multiply it by 24 — for 3.25 days, the 0.25 fraction times 24 gives 6 hours, confirming "3 days and 6 hours" as the full breakdown.

## What this doesn't account for

This conversion treats every day as exactly 24 hours. In regions with daylight saving time, the actual calendar day when clocks shift is either 23 or 25 hours — a detail this straightforward conversion doesn't adjust for, since it's based on the fixed mathematical definition of a day rather than a specific real calendar date.
