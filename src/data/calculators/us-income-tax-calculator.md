# US Income Tax Calculator – Tax Year 2024 Federal Tax Guide

Understanding your US federal tax liability requires applying the official **IRS progressive tax brackets and statutory deductions**.

This calculator estimates your **taxable income, federal tax due, marginal tax rate, effective tax rate, and net take-home pay** based on **Tax Year 2024** rules.

---

### Tax Year 2024 Statutory Framework

#### 1. Standard Deductions (Tax Year 2024)
- **Single Filer**: **$14,600**
- **Married Filing Jointly**: **$29,200**

#### 2. Federal Tax Brackets (Tax Year 2024)

| Tax Rate | Single Filer Taxable Income | Married Joint Filer Taxable Income |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10%** | Up to 11,600 | Up to 23,200 |
| **12%** | 11,601 to 47,150 | 23,201 to 94,300 |
| **22%** | 47,151 to 100,525 | 94,301 to 201,050 |
| **24%** | 100,526 to 191,950 | 201,051 to 383,900 |
| **32%** | 191,951 to 243,725 | 383,901 to 487,450 |
| **35%** | 243,726 to 609,350 | 487,451 to 731,200 |
| **37%** | Over 609,350 | Over 731,200 |

---

### Progressive Tax Calculation Methodology

1. **Determine Taxable Income**:
 Taxable Income = max(0, Gross Annual Income - Chosen Deduction)
2. **Calculate Progressive Tax Across Brackets**:
 Federal Tax = sum_{i=1}^k (Taxable Income in Bracket  i × Rate_i)
3. **Compute Effective & Marginal Tax Rates**:
 Effective Tax Rate = frac(Federal Tax Due){Gross Income} × 100
 Marginal Rate = Tax percentage of the highest active bracket

---

### Worked Numerical Example (Single Filer, 85,000 Gross Income)

1. **Gross Income**: **$85,000**
2. **Standard Deduction (Single 2024)**: **$14,600**
3. **Taxable Income**: \85,000 - $14,600 = $70,400
4. **Bracket Breakdown**:
 - **10% Bracket** (on first 11,600): \11,600 × 0.10 = $1,160.00
 - **12% Bracket** (on 11,601 to 47,150 = 35,550): \35,550 × 0.12 = $4,266.00
 - **22% Bracket** (on 47,151 to 70,400 = 23,250): \23,250 × 0.22 = $5,115.00
5. **Total Federal Income Tax Due**: \1,160.00 + $4,266.00 + $5,115.00 = $10,541.00
6. **Effective Tax Rate**: (\10,541.00 / $85,000) × 100 = 12.40% 7. **Marginal Tax Rate**: **22%**
8. **Net Take-Home Pay**: \85,000 - $10,541 = $74,459

### Important Scope Disclosures

- **Federal Tax Only**: This calculator computes federal individual income tax. It does not include state income taxes, local taxes, or FICA payroll taxes (Social Security 6.2% and Medicare 1.45%).
- **Excludes Custom Credits**: Child Tax Credits, Earned Income Tax Credits (EITC), and tax adjustments are not subtracted from this basic federal model.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Why is my effective tax rate lower than my tax bracket?**
  * A1: The US uses progressive tax brackets. Only income within each specific dollar range is taxed at that bracket's rate. Your lower income portions are taxed at 10% and 12%, making your overall effective rate (12.40%) much lower than your top marginal rate (22%).

* **Q2: Should I take the Standard Deduction or Itemize?**
  * A2: Take the standard deduction ($14,600 Single / 29,200 Married) unless your allowable itemized deductions (mortgage interest, state tax up to 10k, charitable gifts) exceed the standard deduction threshold.
