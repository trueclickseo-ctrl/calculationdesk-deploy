# Tie Length Calculator

Enter your height and preferred knot style, and this calculator recommends a necktie length and size classification.

## Formula

**Base length**: 61 inches if height is over 6'0" (72 in), 54 inches if under 5'5" (65 in), otherwise 57 inches (standard). **Knot adjustment**: +0 inches for Four-in-Hand, +1 inch for Half Windsor, +3 inches for Full Windsor. **Recommended length = Base length + Knot adjustment**.

For someone 6'1" (73 inches) tying a Full Windsor knot: base length is 61 inches (above the tall threshold), plus a 3-inch Full Windsor adjustment, giving a recommended length of 64 inches — classified as Extra-Long (XL).

## Why knot style matters as much as height

A bulkier knot uses noticeably more fabric in its wraps before the rest of the tie reaches its intended position. A slim Four-in-Hand knot needs no extra length beyond the height-based base, a Half Windsor adds a modest amount, and a Full Windsor — with its larger, more structured triangular shape — adds the most. In practice, switching knot styles can shift the recommended length by as much as moving into a different height bracket would.

## Where the tie should actually sit

The generally accepted rule is that the tip of the tie should land at the middle of the belt buckle when standing upright. This calculator's recommendation is aimed at achieving that placement, though final fit also depends on factors it doesn't measure directly, like neck circumference — a larger neck uses up more length in the collar itself, so it's worth sizing up slightly if yours runs larger than average.
