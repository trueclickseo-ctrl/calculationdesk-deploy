# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) Calculator – Girl Child Savings Guide

The **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)** is a sovereign-backed savings scheme launched under the *Beti Bachao Beti Padhao* campaign. It provides high interest returns and full tax exemptions (EEE status) to build a dedicated financial fund for a girl child's education and marriage.

---

### How the SSY Calculation Engine Works

SSY accounts run for **21 years from account opening**, divided into two distinct phases:

1. **Contribution Phase (Years 1 to 15)**: You deposit your chosen annual amount ($P$) at the start of each year. The balance compounds annually at interest rate $R$:
   $$\text{Balance}_t = (\text{Balance}_{t-1} + P) \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)$$
2. **Growth-Only Phase (Years 16 to 21)**: No new deposits are accepted. Your accumulated balance continues to compound for the final 6 years:
   $$\text{Balance}_t = \text{Balance}_{t-1} \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)$$

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Example (Verified against Code)

Suppose you open an account for a **1-year-old child**, depositing **₹50,000 annually** at an interest rate of **8.2% p.a.**:

1. **Contribution Phase (Years 1 to 15)**:
   - Annual Deposit: **₹50,000**
   - Total Principal Deposited: $₹50,000 \times 15 = \mathbf{₹7,50,000}$
   - Balance accumulated at end of Year 15: **₹14,94,287**
2. **Growth Phase (Years 16 to 21)**:
   - Balance compounds from ₹14,94,287 for 6 additional years at 8.2%:
   $$\text{Maturity Value} = 14,94,287 \times (1.082)^6 \approx \mathbf{₹23,97,423}$$
3. **Summary**:
   - **Total Principal Invested**: **₹7,50,000**
   - **Interest Yield Earned**: $\$23,97,423 - \$7,50,000 = \mathbf{₹16,47,423}$
   - **Total Tax-Free Maturity Corpus**: **₹23,97,423**

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: What are the minimum and maximum annual deposit limits?**
  * A1: The statutory minimum deposit is ₹250 per year, and the maximum deposit is ₹1,50,000 per financial year. Deposits above ₹1.5 Lakhs earn no interest.
