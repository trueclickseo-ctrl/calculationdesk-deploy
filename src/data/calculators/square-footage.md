# Square Footage Calculator

Enter a space's length and width, and this calculator returns the total area in square feet.

## Formula

**Area (sq ft) = Length × Width**

For a 12 ft by 10 ft room: Area = 12 × 10 = 120 sq ft.

## Handling rooms that aren't simple rectangles

The formula itself only works cleanly on a true rectangle, but most real rooms — L-shapes, spaces with closets or alcoves, anything with a jog in the wall — can still be measured this way by breaking the space into two or more rectangular sections. Calculate each section's area separately with this same formula, then add them together for the accurate total, rather than trying to force one length and width measurement to describe an irregular shape.

## Why the "real" number for ordering materials is usually higher

The raw square footage this calculator returns is the exact floor area, but it's rarely the exact quantity to order when buying flooring, tile, or carpet. Materials are sold in fixed-size units that don't divide evenly into a room's actual dimensions, and cutting pieces to fit around edges and obstacles wastes material that can't be reused elsewhere. Padding the raw square footage by around 10% — more for complex layouts or patterned installations — is standard practice to avoid running short mid-project.
