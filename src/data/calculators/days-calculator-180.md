# 180 Day Calculator

Enter a starting date, and this calculator instantly finds the exact calendar date 180 days after and 180 days before it.

## Formula

**Future date = Starting date + 180 days**. **Past date = Starting date − 180 days**. The calculation works from actual calendar date objects, so it correctly handles month-length differences and leap years.

For January 1, 2026: 180 days in the future is June 30, 2026, and 180 days in the past is July 5, 2025.

## The Schengen 90/180 rule — and why this tool isn't a full tracker for it

Many non-EU travelers to the Schengen Area are limited to 90 days within any rolling 180-day period — a window that shifts forward continuously rather than sitting fixed to one starting date. Correctly tracking that rule means counting actual travel days across a moving 180-day span, which is a different (and more involved) calculation than this tool's single fixed offset. This calculator is well suited to a one-time 180-day deadline, not an ongoing rolling-window count.

## 180 days vs. "six months"

These are close but not identical — six consecutive calendar months typically run 181 to 184 days depending on the specific months involved, so a fixed 180-day count usually lands a bit earlier than "six months from now." The gap is small enough that the two get used interchangeably in casual conversation, but for anything deadline-sensitive, the exact day count is what matters.

## Other common 180-day periods

Six-month medical and dental checkup reminders, many state residency determination rules for tax purposes, some COBRA health coverage notice periods, and various lease or loan renewal notice requirements all commonly use a 180-day interval.
