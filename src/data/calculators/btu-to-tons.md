# BTU to Tons Calculator

Enter a cooling system's BTU-per-hour rating, and this calculator converts it to tons of refrigeration — the unit most residential central air conditioners are sized and sold in.

## Formula

**Tons = BTUs per Hour ÷ 12,000**

For a 24,000 BTU/hr system: Tons = 24000 ÷ 12000 = 2.0 tons.

## Where the "ton" of cooling comes from

The unit predates mechanical air conditioning entirely. A ton of refrigeration was originally defined as the cooling rate needed to melt one short ton of ice over 24 hours — a figure that works out to 12,000 BTU per hour. Even though nobody cools buildings with ice blocks anymore, the unit stuck, and it remains the standard way residential and light commercial HVAC equipment is specified today.

## Why the right size matters more than the biggest number

Bigger isn't automatically better when it comes to matching a system's tonnage to a space. An oversized unit cools quickly but shuts off before it's run long enough to properly remove humidity, leading to short, frequent cycles and a space that feels cool but damp. An undersized unit, conversely, runs nearly nonstop and still may not reach the target temperature on the hottest days. A proper load calculation — not just a square-footage guess — is the right way to determine the tonnage a specific space actually needs.
