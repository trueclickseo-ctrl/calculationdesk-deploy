# Simple Interest Calculator – Linear Interest Guide

**Simple Interest** is a basic method of calculating interest where returns or charges are calculated as a fixed percentage of the original principal amount. The interest earned in one period is not added back to the principal, so returns grow linearly over time.

---

### How the Calculation Engine Works

The calculator converts your time period into fractional years ($t$) based on your chosen unit (Years, Months, or Days).

#### Mathematical Simple Interest Formula

$$I = P \times \left(\frac{R}{100}\right) \times t$$

$$\text{Maturity Value } A = P + I$$

Where:
- **$P$**: Principal investment or loan balance.
- **$R$**: Quoted annual simple interest rate (%).
- **$t$**: Time tenure in years:
  - If Years: $t = \text{Tenure}$
  - If Months: $t = \frac{\text{Tenure}}{12}$
  - If Days: $t = \frac{\text{Tenure}}{365}$

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Example (Verified against Code)

Suppose you invest **$10,000** for **18 months** at an annual simple interest rate of **6%**:

1. **Convert Months to Fractional Years ($t$)**:
   $$t = \frac{18}{12} = 1.5\text{ years}$$
2. **Calculate Simple Interest ($I$)**:
   $$I = 10,000 \times \left(\frac{6}{100}\right) \times 1.5 = 10,000 \times 0.06 \times 1.5 = \mathbf{\$900.00}$$
3. **Calculate Final Maturity Value ($A$)**:
   $$A = \$10,000 + \$900 = \mathbf{\$10,900.00}$$
4. **Summary**:
   - **Original Principal**: **$10,000**
   - **Total Simple Interest**: **$900**
   - **Final Maturity Value**: **$10,900**
