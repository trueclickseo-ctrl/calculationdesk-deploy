# Salary to Hourly Rate Calculator

Enter an annual gross salary and typical weekly work hours, and this calculator returns the equivalent gross hourly pay rate.

## Formula

**Hourly Rate = Gross Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × 52)**

For a $52,000 salary at 40 hours per week: Hourly = 52,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = 52,000 ÷ 2,080 = $25.00/hr.

## Why 52 weeks, not fewer

Dividing by all 52 weeks of the year — rather than subtracting vacation weeks first — reflects how a true salary actually works: it's a fixed annual amount, paid consistently whether or not the employee takes their allotted time off. This is a meaningful difference from hourly work, where taking unpaid time off directly reduces total earnings for the year, since hourly pay is tied strictly to hours actually worked.

## What this number doesn't include

This is a gross, pre-tax figure — actual take-home hourly value will be lower once income tax and other payroll deductions come out of each paycheck, and exactly how much lower depends on individual tax circumstances this calculator doesn't attempt to estimate. It's also worth remembering that salaried roles often bundle in benefits, paid holidays, and retirement contributions that a bare hourly-rate comparison against an hourly job offer won't capture — the full compensation picture is usually broader than this one number.
