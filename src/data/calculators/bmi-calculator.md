# BMI Calculator – Body Mass Index & Weight Status Guide

**Body Mass Index (BMI)** is a widely recognized health screening metric used by medical professionals, fitness trainers, and researchers to assess whether an adult's weight is proportionate to their height.

---

### Correct BMI Formulas

#### 1. Metric Units (Kilograms & Meters)

$$BMI = \frac{\text{Weight (kg)}}{\text{Height (m)}^2}$$

#### 2. Imperial Units (Pounds & Inches)

$$BMI = 703 \times \frac{\text{Weight (lbs)}}{\text{Height (inches)}^2}$$

---

### Worked BMI Example with Actual Numbers

Let's calculate the BMI of an adult who is **175 cm (1.75 meters) tall** and weighs **70 kg**:

1. **Calculate Height in Meters Squared**:
   $$\text{Height}^2 = 1.75 \times 1.75 = 3.0625\text{ m}^2$$
2. **Divide Weight by Height Squared**:
   $$BMI = \frac{70}{3.0625} \approx 22.86$$
3. **Assessment**: A BMI of **22.9** falls within the **Normal weight** category (**18.5 to 24.9**).

---

### World Health Organization (WHO) BMI Categories

| BMI Range | Weight Classification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Below 18.5** | Underweight |
| **18.5 – 24.9** | **Normal Weight (Healthy)** |
| **25.0 – 29.9** | Overweight |
| **30.0 or Higher** | Obese |

---

### What BMI Can and Cannot Tell You

#### What BMI Tells You:
- Provides a fast, population-level screening tool for weight status.
- Helps identify potential health risks associated with underweight or obesity.

#### Limitations of BMI (What it Cannot Measure):
1. **Muscle vs. Fat**: BMI cannot differentiate between muscle mass and adipose fat. Athletes and bodybuilders often score in the "overweight" or "obese" ranges despite having exceptionally low body fat.
2. **Age & Gender Variance**: Women naturally carry a higher percentage of body fat than men at identical BMI values. Elderly individuals may lose muscle mass while retaining weight, hiding fat accumulation.
3. **Fat Distribution**: BMI does not measure visceral fat stored around abdominal organs, which carries higher cardiovascular risks than subcutaneous fat.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Should children use this adult BMI calculator?**
  * A1: No. Child and adolescent BMI requires percentile charts based on age and sex growth patterns published by health authorities (CDC / WHO).

* **Q2: What is the healthy weight range for someone 5 ft 9 in (175 cm) tall?**
  * A2: A healthy weight (BMI 18.5 to 24.9) for a 175 cm tall person is approximately **56.7 kg to 76.3 kg (125 lbs to 168 lbs)**.

* **Q3: What is Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)?**
  * A3: BMR is the estimated number of calories your body burns every day just to stay alive at complete rest.
