# Semester GPA Calculator

This calculates your GPA for a single term by weighting each course's grade points by how many credit hours it's worth — the same method most schools use for both semester and cumulative GPA.

## Why credit hours matter

A straight average of your grades would treat a 1-credit seminar the same as a 4-credit core course, which doesn't reflect how much each one actually contributes to your academic record. Weighting by credit hours fixes that: a 4-credit course with a B pulls more weight in your GPA than a 1-credit course with an A, because it represents more of your term.

## The formula

**Semester GPA = Σ(grade points × credit hours) / Σ(credit hours)**. Multiply each course's grade point value by its credit hours to get that course's "quality points," add up all the quality points, then divide by the total credit hours for the term. Four courses graded 4.0, 3.0, 3.7, and 2.7 at 3, 4, 3, and 2 credits respectively work out to 40.5 total quality points over 12 total credits — a 3.375 GPA.

## Semester GPA vs. cumulative GPA

These are two different numbers answering two different questions. Semester GPA reflects only the current term; cumulative GPA weighs every credit hour you've earned across your entire academic record. A single strong or weak semester moves your semester GPA a lot, but it moves your cumulative GPA much less, simply because it's diluted across a much larger pool of credit hours.
