# CGPA Calculator – Cumulative Semester Performance Guide

Evaluating overall academic standing across multiple college or university terms requires calculating your **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)**. This calculator computes your overall cumulative CGPA, total semesters evaluated, and equivalent percentage score.

## How the CGPA Calculation Works

### 1. Cumulative CGPA (Arithmetic Average)

$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n} \text{GPA}_k}{n}$$

Where **GPA_k** is the grade point average achieved in semester *k*, and **n** is the total number of semesters evaluated.

### 2. Equivalent Percentage Conversion (CBSE Scale)

$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = \text{CGPA} \times 9.5$$

The 9.5 multiplier is the standard benchmark established by educational boards like CBSE. Institutional rules may vary, so this figure should be treated as an estimate.

## Worked Example

Suppose a student completes **4 semesters** with GPAs of **8.5, 7.8, 8.2,** and **9.0**:

1. Total GPA sum: $8.5 + 7.8 + 8.2 + 9.0 = 33.5$
2. Cumulative CGPA: $33.5 \div 4 = 8.38 / 10.0$
3. Equivalent percentage: $8.375 \times 9.5 \approx 79.61\%$

## How More Semesters Change the Average

Adding more semesters to the calculation simply extends the arithmetic mean — a stronger run of recent semesters can pull the cumulative score up over time:

| Semesters | GPAs Entered | Cumulative CGPA | Equivalent Percentage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 8.5, 7.8, 8.2, 9.0 | 8.38 / 10.0 | 79.61% |
| 6 | 9.2, 8.9, 9.5, 8.7, 9.0, 8.4 | 8.95 / 10.0 | 85.02% |

## What This Calculator Does Not Include

> [!NOTE]
> **Real-world exclusions**: This calculator treats every semester as equally weighted. If your institution assigns different credit hours per semester, a credit-weighted average — $\sum (\text{SGPA} \times \text{Credits}) / \sum \text{Credits}$ — would be more accurate than this simple mean. It also uses the CBSE 9.5 multiplier by default, which not every institution follows.
