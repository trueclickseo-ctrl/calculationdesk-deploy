# Area Converter – m², ft², Acres, Hectares & More

### Area Versus Length — An Important Distinction

Before diving into specific conversions, it helps to understand why area unit factors are not the same as length unit factors. Area measures a two-dimensional surface: it is the product of two lengths. So when you move from one measurement system to another, you need to apply the length conversion factor twice — once for each dimension.

One meter equals 3.28084 feet. But one square meter does not equal 3.28084 square feet. It equals 3.28084 × 3.28084 = 10.7639 square feet. This squared relationship applies to every area conversion. A room that is 5 meters by 4 meters has an area of 20 m² — which is 20 × 10.7639 = 215.3 ft², not 20 × 3.28 = 65.6 ft².

### The Units This Converter Supports

The calculator works across ten area units, bridging metric and imperial measurements:

**Metric units:** Square millimeters (mm²) for very small surfaces like cross-sections in engineering diagrams. Square centimeters (cm²) for paper, screens, and small product dimensions. Square meters (m²) for room sizes, apartment floor plans, and building layouts — the everyday metric area unit. Square kilometers (km²) for large geographical areas: cities, national parks, countries. Hectares (ha) for agricultural and land planning — 1 ha = 10,000 m².

**Imperial and US customary units:** Square inches (in²) for products, tiles, and technical drawings. Square feet (ft²) for room sizes, real estate listings, and property in the US and UK. Square yards (yd²) for carpeting, flooring, and fabric in imperial-using countries. Square miles (mi²) for large land areas in the US. Acres (ac) for land, farms, and real estate plots — one acre = 43,560 ft².

### How the Converter Works

All conversions go through square meters as a common reference. The input is multiplied by the m² factor for its unit to produce a total in m². That total is then divided by the m² factor of the target unit. For example, converting 5 acres to hectares:

1. 5 × 4,046.8564224 = 20,234.28 m²
2. 20,234.28 / 10,000 = **2.023 ha**

The same method works for any pairing of units without a dedicated formula for every combination.

### A Fourth Worked Example (3 Hectares to Acres and Square Feet)

Farmland is often listed in hectares outside the US, but US buyers usually think in acres or square feet. Converting **3 hectares**:

1. Convert to square meters: 3 × 10,000 = **30,000 m²**
2. Convert to acres: 30,000 / 4,046.8564224 = **7.413 acres**
3. Convert to square feet: 30,000 / 0.09290304 = **322,917.31 ft²**

### Practical Use Cases

**Real estate:** US listings use square feet; European listings use square meters. Converting 1,500 ft² to m² gives 1,500 × 0.09290304 = 139.4 m² — useful when comparing properties across markets.

**Agriculture and land:** Farmland in the US is measured in acres; in Europe and Australia, hectares are standard. A 200-acre farm is 200 × 0.404686 = 80.94 hectares.

**Construction:** Flooring, roofing, and tiling projects often mix units — a room might be measured in feet but tile sold per m². Getting the conversion right prevents ordering too much or too little material.

**Landscaping and gardening:** Seed, fertilizer, and irrigation coverage rates are often given per acre or per hectare, while the actual plot may be measured in square meters.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How many square feet are in an acre?**

There are exactly 43,560 square feet in one acre. This is a precise definition: 1 acre = 4,046.8564224 m², and 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m², so 4046.8564224 / 0.09290304 = 43,560. When buying rural property in the US, most acreage is described in feet and acres, so knowing this equivalence is essential.

**Q2: What is the difference between a hectare and an acre?**

A hectare is a metric unit equal to 10,000 m² — it is essentially a 100m × 100m square of land. An acre is an imperial unit equal to 4,046.856 m². One hectare equals 2.471 acres, or conversely, 1 acre is about 0.405 hectares. Hectares are standard across Europe, Australia, and most of the world; acres are primarily used in the US and UK for land.

**Q3: Why are area conversion factors different from length conversion factors?**

Because area is length squared. One foot = 0.3048 meters. But one square foot = 0.3048 × 0.3048 = 0.09290304 square meters. You cannot simply use the length factor for area. Similarly, 1 km = 1,000 m, but 1 km² = 1,000 × 1,000 = 1,000,000 m².

**Q4: What is the difference between square feet and feet?**

Feet (ft) measure length — one-dimensional distance. Square feet (ft²) measure area — a two-dimensional surface. A room 15 ft long is not 15 ft² in area; if it is also 12 ft wide, its area is 15 × 12 = 180 ft². This is perhaps the most common mistake in home measurement.

**Q5: How many square meters are in a square kilometer?**

Exactly 1,000,000 m². One kilometer = 1,000 meters, so 1 km² = 1,000 × 1,000 = 1,000,000 m². This means converting between m² and km² is simply a matter of moving the decimal point 6 places.
