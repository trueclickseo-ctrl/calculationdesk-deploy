# Limestone Calculator

Enter the length, width, and desired depth of a project area, and this calculator returns the volume and weight of crushed limestone needed.

## Formula

**Cubic Yards = (Length × Width × (Depth in inches ÷ 12)) ÷ 27**, then **Tons = Cubic Yards × 1.4**

For a driveway 50 ft long, 10 ft wide, and 4 inches deep: Yards = (50 × 10 × (4/12)) ÷ 27 ≈ 6.17; Tons = 6.17 × 1.4 ≈ 8.64.

## Why crushed limestone is the standard driveway subbase choice

Crushed limestone's angular, jagged pieces interlock and compact far more tightly than naturally rounded gravel, creating a stable, well-draining base layer that resists rutting and shifting under vehicle weight. That's why it's the default recommendation for driveway subbases, typically laid 4 to 6 inches deep depending on soil conditions and expected traffic load.

## Two numbers, two different purposes

This calculator returns both cubic yards and tons because suppliers usually price and deliver crushed stone by weight, while the project itself is planned around area and depth. Cubic yards tells you how much physical coverage you're getting; tons tells you what it'll cost and how much truck capacity a delivery will need. Keep in mind the 1.4 tons-per-yard conversion is a standard estimate — actual density varies somewhat by the specific stone size and source, so confirming with a supplier refines the final order quantity.
