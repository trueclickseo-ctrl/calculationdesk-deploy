# Stamp Duty Calculator – Estimate Property Transfer & Registration Costs

When purchasing real estate, the purchase price is not the only cash requirement. Governments impose mandatory transfer duties and legal registration charges when property ownership changes hands.

This calculator computes **Stamp Duty Tax**, **Registration Fees**, and **Total Acquisition Capital Outlay** based on user-entered percentage rates.

---

### Mathematical Formulas

#### 1. Stamp Duty Tax ($S$)
$$S = \text{Property Purchase Price} \times \left( \frac{\text{Duty Rate \%}}{100} \right)$$

#### 2. Registration Fee ($R$)
$$R = \text{Property Purchase Price} \times \left( \frac{\text{Registration Rate \%}}{100} \right)$$

#### 3. Total Capital Outlay ($T$)
$$T = \text{Property Purchase Price} + S + R$$

### Verified Step-by-Step Worked Example

Let's calculate property acquisition fees for a **$300,000 property** with a **4.0% stamp duty rate** and a **1.0% registration rate**:

#### Step 1: Calculate Stamp Duty Tax
$$S = \$300,000 \times \left( \frac{4.0}{100} \right) = \$300,000 \times 0.04 = \mathbf{\$12,000}$$

#### Step 2: Calculate Registration Fees
$$R = \$300,000 \times \left( \frac{1.0}{100} \right) = \$300,000 \times 0.01 = \mathbf{\$3,000}$$

#### Step 3: Compute Total Capital Outlay
$$T = \$300,000 + \$12,000 + \$3,000 = \mathbf{\$315,000}$$

### Critical Jurisdiction & Legal Caveats

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - **Rate-Based Estimation**: This tool applies flat percentage rates entered by the user. Actual legal tax obligations depend on government revenue schedules.
> - **Progressive Brackets**: Many jurisdictions (such as the UK, Australia, or India) charge progressive stamp duty rates where higher portion slices of the property price are taxed at higher rates.
> - **Exemptions & Relief**: First-time homebuyers often qualify for stamp duty discounts, concessions, or complete tax exemptions under specific price caps.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: Who pays stamp duty—the buyer or the seller?**
  * A1: In almost all real estate transactions, stamp duty and property registration fees are paid by the buyer at settlement.
* **Q2: Does stamp duty vary for commercial vs. residential properties?**
  * A2: Yes. Most tax authorities charge different duty rates for residential homes, commercial buildings, vacant land, and investor purchases.
* **Q3: How do I verify my exact government stamp duty liability?**
  * A3: Check the official website of your local state revenue office, land registry department, or consult with a licensed real estate conveyancer or attorney.
