# Kilometers to Miles Converter

One kilometer equals 0.621371192 miles — a figure that comes directly from the definition of the international mile as exactly 1,609.344 meters. Enter a distance in kilometers to get the equivalent in miles.

## Quick reference points

For a fast mental estimate, multiply kilometers by 0.6, or divide by 8 and multiply by 5 — 8 km is close enough to 5 miles that the shortcut holds up well for everyday distances.

| Kilometers | Miles | Common Reference |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 km | 0.62 mi | — |
| 5 km | 3.11 mi | 5K race |
| 10 km | 6.21 mi | 10K race |
| 21.1 km | 13.11 mi | Half marathon |
| 42.2 km | 26.22 mi | Full marathon |
| 100 km | 62.14 mi | — |

The same factor converts speed: a 100 km/h speed limit is about 62 mph, which is why European rental cars often list both units on the speedometer.
