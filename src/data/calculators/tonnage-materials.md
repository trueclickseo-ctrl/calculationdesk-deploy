# Material Tonnage Calculator

Enter a material's volume and bulk density, and this calculator returns its total weight in short tons.

## Formula

**Tonnage (short tons) = (Volume (cu ft) × Density (lbs/cu ft)) ÷ 2,000**

For 100 cubic feet of material at 100 lbs/cu ft: Tonnage = (100 × 100) ÷ 2000 = 5.0 tons.

## Why the density figure matters more than the formula

The formula itself is simple, but the accuracy of the result rides entirely on using the right density for the actual material in question. Dry sand, gravel, topsoil, and mulch all have meaningfully different bulk densities, and even the same material can weigh more or less depending on how wet or compacted it is. Plugging in a generic assumed density instead of a supplier-confirmed figure for the specific material is the most common way this kind of estimate goes wrong.

## A note on which "ton" you mean

This calculator uses the short ton (2,000 lbs), the standard unit for US construction and landscaping supply. It's not the only "ton" in circulation — the long ton (2,240 lbs) and the metric tonne (2,204.6 lbs) are both used elsewhere, and mixing them up when comparing quotes or delivery tickets can introduce a meaningful discrepancy, especially on larger orders.
