# Weeks to Years Converter

Enter a duration in weeks, and this calculator converts it into the equivalent number of years.

## Formula

**Years = Weeks ÷ 52.1775**, where 52.1775 is the average number of weeks in a Gregorian calendar year (365.2425 days ÷ 7).

150 weeks converts to 150 ÷ 52.1775 ≈ 2.875 years — about 2 years, 10 months, and 2 weeks.

## Why not just divide by 52?

52 weeks is only 364 days, one day short of a standard 365-day year. Using a plain 52 as the divisor would introduce a small error that compounds over longer durations. The 52.1775 figure instead reflects the true average calendar year length across the full leap year cycle, giving a more accurate conversion for multi-year spans.

## Pregnancy and other common week-based measures

Standard pregnancy duration, commonly tracked as 40 weeks, converts to roughly 0.77 years — close to the "9 months" figure often cited, since 9 months works out to about 0.75 years. This average-based conversion is well suited to that kind of duration tracking, though for measuring the exact span between two specific calendar dates, a date-difference calculator using real dates will be more precise.
