# Marks Percentage Calculator – Test & Exam Grade Converter

Calculating a percentage score on a test or exam requires comparing the marks earned against the total maximum marks available. This calculator computes the obtained score percentage, marks lost, and percentage lost.

## How the Marks Percentage Formulas Work

$$\text{Percentage (\%)} = \frac{M_{\text{obtained}}}{M_{\text{total}}} \times 100$$

$$\text{Marks Lost} = M_{\text{total}} - M_{\text{obtained}} \qquad \text{Marks Lost (\%)} = \frac{\text{Marks Lost}}{M_{\text{total}}} \times 100$$

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Standard Score (425 out of 500)

1. Percentage: $\dfrac{425}{500} \times 100 = 85.00\%$
2. Marks lost: $500 - 425 = 75$ marks
3. Marks lost percentage: $\dfrac{75}{500} \times 100 = 15.0\%$

### Example 2: Non-Round Fraction Score (73 out of 87)

1. Percentage: $\dfrac{73}{87} \times 100 \approx 83.91\%$
2. Marks lost: $87 - 73 = 14$ marks
3. Marks lost percentage: $\dfrac{14}{87} \times 100 \approx 16.09\%$

## Edge Cases and Special Inputs

- **Perfect score** (obtained = total): scoring 500 out of 500 yields a **100.00%** score with **0 marks lost**.
- **Zero score** (obtained = 0): scoring 0 out of 500 yields a **0.00%** score with **500 marks lost** (100% lost).
- **Validation rules**: total marks must be greater than zero, and obtained marks cannot be negative.

## What This Calculator Does Not Include

> [!NOTE]
> **Real-world exclusions**: This calculator converts a single test's raw score into a percentage. It does not combine multiple tests of different weights into one overall percentage — for that, sum every test's obtained marks and every test's total marks separately before dividing.
