# Marks Percentage Calculator – Test & Exam Grade Converter

Calculating your **percentage score** on a test or exam requires comparing the marks you earned against the total maximum marks available.

This calculator computes your **obtained score percentage, marks lost, and percentage lost**.

---

### How the Marks Percentage Formulas Work

1. **Obtained Score Percentage**:
   Percentage (%) = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) × 100

2. **Marks Lost**:
   Marks Lost = Total Marks − Marks Obtained

3. **Marks Lost Percentage**:
   Marks Lost (%) = (Marks Lost / Total Marks) × 100

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples (Verified against Code)

#### Example 1: Standard Score (425 out of 500)
1. **Inputs**: Marks Obtained = 425, Total Marks = 500.
2. **Calculate Percentage**:
   Percentage = (425 / 500) × 100 = 0.85 × 100 = **85.00%**
3. **Calculate Marks Lost**:
   Marks Lost = 500 − 425 = **75 marks**
4. **Calculate Marks Lost Percentage**:
   Marks Lost % = (75 / 500) × 100 = **15.0%**

#### Example 2: Non-Round Fraction Score (73 out of 87)
1. **Inputs**: Marks Obtained = 73, Total Marks = 87.
2. **Calculate Percentage**:
   Percentage = (73 / 87) × 100 = 0.839080 × 100 = **83.91%**
3. **Calculate Marks Lost**:
   Marks Lost = 87 − 73 = **14 marks** (16.09%)

---

### Edge Cases and Special Inputs

* **Perfect Score (Obtained = Total)**: Scoring 500 out of 500 yields a **100.00%** score with **0 marks lost**.
* **Zero Score (Obtained = 0)**: Scoring 0 out of 500 yields a **0.00%** score with **500 marks lost (100% lost)**.
* **Validation Rules**: Total marks must be greater than zero, and obtained marks cannot be negative.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q1: How is the marks percentage calculated?**
  * A1: Divide your obtained marks by the total maximum marks and multiply by 100: Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) × 100.
* **Q2: How are marks lost calculated?**
  * A2: Marks lost is the difference between total maximum marks and marks obtained: Marks Lost = Total Marks − Marks Obtained.
